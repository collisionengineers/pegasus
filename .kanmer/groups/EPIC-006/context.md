# Shared context

This epic is the end-to-end email-management workspace. Preserve the Core-owned classification policy and evidence taxonomy established by MAIL-21/MAIL-22. Mailbox and Outlook writes remain behind explicit confirmation and the repository live-operation approval rules; local-alpha work must not mutate a real Outlook mailbox. UI, Core policy, infrastructure adapters, and Automation Actor callers must reuse one canonical business implementation. Archived/superseded tickets and separate evaluation, report-sending, and chasing capabilities are outside this epic.
