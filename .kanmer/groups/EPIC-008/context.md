# Shared scope

This epic is the requested redesign programme for the Case and Operations workspaces and the named Administration surfaces. Deliver concise operator-facing pages in line with `docs/design/README.md`; do not add explanatory copy. Treat every ticket as a frontend-led investigation: when it reveals an unmet backend capability or process, capture that as a distinct linked follow-up ticket rather than silently expanding the current ticket.

The requested consolidation is deliberate: Staff accounts, Staff roles, and Access Review form one ticket; Organizations and Principals form one ticket.
