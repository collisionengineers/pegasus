# Research — TICK-069: EXT-15 automated WhatsApp ingestion and coexistence

## Question

What requirements and existing seams govern automated WhatsApp ingestion, what is absent today, and what must be decided and evidenced before EXT-15 can be planned for implementation?

## Findings

- **EXT-15 is allocation, not activation.** `docs/capabilities.md` assigns “Automated WhatsApp ingestion and coexistence” to Later / 0.5.0 and says the owning channel, custody, identity, and coexistence evidence are still required. The ticket is labelled post-alpha, blocked, and requires-live-approval.
- **The operator truth establishes WhatsApp as an authoritative intake channel, but not an automated integration.** `docs/operator-notes.md#authoritative-channels-and-formats` accepts PDF, DOC/DOCX, and typed text through WhatsApp. `docs/operator-notes.md#external-systems` says it is primarily used to chase garages for images, frequently receives images, and temporarily stages unmatched images on a network drive. The same section explicitly says the supplied sources do not prove automated WhatsApp ingestion or staging-to-Box transfer.
- **Coexistence must preserve the manual route.** Operator truth requires definitive automatic linking and staff-controlled manual linking for image-led work. `docs/frd/frd-05-documents-extraction-and-custody.md` already permits staff to add manually received WhatsApp evidence with source/channel provenance and says this does not activate a WhatsApp integration. Automation must therefore coexist with, not reinterpret or remove, manual receipt and network-drive staging until an accepted migration/cutover decision says otherwise.
- **The governing behavioural baseline is FRD-02.** `docs/frd/frd-02-intake-and-source-identity.md` requires every intake path to retain original bytes and available transport metadata; distinguish occurrence identity from content hash; keep equal bytes received as separate permitted occurrences; reject occurrence-token reuse with different bytes; durably commit source, receipt, and dispatch before acknowledgement; make duplicate delivery idempotent; surface corrupt, unsupported, oversized, ambiguous, and technical failures; preserve actor/caller/source/policy/reason history; and prevent untrusted content becoming authority.
- **Case association remains fail closed.** FRD-02 requires successful persistence and extraction, authenticated Principal/actor where applicable, provider/intermediary route identity and enabled policy, unambiguous case type and Principal, limits, and no unresolved identity/custody conflict before Case/PO allocation. Weak, conflicting, or ambiguous signals go to Unidentified; arrival-time proximity never establishes association. Manual link/reversal and immutable source provenance remain available.
- **Current custody layers are distinct.** `docs/operator-notes.md#storage-and-staging-interpretation` defines the network drive as temporary staging for unmatched WhatsApp images and Box as long-term case-file custody. FRD-02 makes blob staging temporary processing storage and Box custody post-allocation. Automated receipt must not claim Box custody or case association merely because WhatsApp/provider acknowledgement succeeded.
- **The existing provider-neutral intake path is reusable.** `src/Pegasus.Core/Intake/IntakeContracts.cs` owns `IntakeSourceIdentity`, `IntakeSource`, receipts, evidence, assets, provenance, decisions, and mutation ports. `src/Pegasus.Core/Intake/DurableIntake.cs` owns `ReceiveIntake`, durable staging, hash/identity conflict detection, work dispatch, replay-safe processing, leases, terminal outcomes, and reconciliation. `src/Pegasus.Worker/IntakeFunctions.cs` owns processing of stable identifiers from the existing intake queue. A WhatsApp transport must call these owners rather than duplicate intake policy.
- **The exact channel model is unresolved.** `IntakeSourceChannel` currently has only `ManualUpload`, `Mailbox`, and `Automation`. Research cannot decide whether an approved WhatsApp provider is represented by a new channel value or an existing transport-neutral category until the provider operation, occurrence identity, acknowledgement semantics, and provenance requirements are settled. The one-list-per-concept rule prohibits a second channel taxonomy in an adapter or UI.
- **No WhatsApp runtime surface exists.** Repository search found no WhatsApp client, webhook endpoint, webhook verification, provider queue, credential/options type, composition registration, persistence schema, or tests under `src/` or `tests/`. `docs/current-architecture.md` and `docs/operations.md#deferred-capability-seams` deliberately exclude the client, webhook, and queue before activation.
- **Activation evidence is explicit.** `docs/operations.md#deferred-capability-seams` requires product/provider selection, sandbox approval, versioned webhook/client fixtures, provenance, consent, duplicate and receipt handling. The general deferred-integration rule additionally requires settled product policy, representative evidence, licence/cost/security approval, a real caller, production adapter, contract fixtures, live sandbox where applicable, and rollout/rollback evidence.
- **External writes remain separately authorised.** Repository safety rails permit read-only cloud checks, but any credential, provider, webhook registration, sandbox mutation, external write, or cloud-state change requires explicit approval for exact targets. Local alpha must not mutate Outlook or Box, and no live WhatsApp target has been approved.
- **No live/provider premise was assumed.** The repository and ticket were checked read-only. Provider availability, API direction, webhook behaviour, account ownership, consent model, media-retention rules, costs/licensing, and sandbox capability remain unverified because no product/provider or target is selected.

## Implications

- Planning implementation now would be premature. EXT-15 first needs an activation decision that identifies the product/provider, owning account/channel, supported inbound operation, caller direction, exact occurrence/correlation identity, authentication/webhook verification, consent basis, acknowledgement contract, and coexistence/cutover policy.
- FRD-02 must own the resulting channel behaviour: accepted message/media forms (including images, PDFs, DOC/DOCX, and typed text), transport metadata, grouping/placement semantics, duplicate and replay rules, bounded failures, Unidentified routing, association/reversal, custody transitions, and acceptance evidence. If a durable technical boundary is chosen (for example a public webhook and verification mechanism), record only that mechanism in a thin ADR and keep behaviour in FRD-02.
- Reuse `ReceiveIntake` and the existing durable Worker pipeline. Add a provider adapter and composition-root caller only after the contract is accepted; do not create a second intake engine, dormant flag/route, top-level project, store, runtime, migration stream, or deployment unit merely to reserve the capability.
- Coexistence acceptance must prove that manual WhatsApp additions and temporary unmatched-image staging continue safely during rollout; automated duplicates across manual and provider routes remain separately attributable unless the accepted occurrence model proves they are the same occurrence; rollback must leave retained material and manual association usable.
- Contract and acceptance tests must cover signature/authentication failure, replay, same occurrence/same bytes, same occurrence/different bytes, equal bytes/different occurrences, message edits/deletes where exposed, multi-image and mixed-media grouping, filenames/content types/ordering, oversized/corrupt/encrypted/unsupported media, provider throttling/outage, redelivery after timeout, ambiguous Principal/case matching, manual link/reversal, custody failure, and bounded non-disclosing operator status.

## Open questions

All unresolved activation questions are recorded in `open-questions` and explicitly parked until EXT-15 is promoted from Later/post-alpha.
