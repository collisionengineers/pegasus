## Why — operator direction (2026-08-22)

> "We do not need a whole section for DVSA/DVLA data. We do not need the entire MOT history. We mainly just need the double lookup to populate vehicle details if the instructions or original report don't have them, and to get a mileage if the original documents did not provide one."

## What the lookups are for

Two jobs, both gap-filling:

1. **Vehicle details** — make, model and the rest, when the instruction and the original report do not carry them.
2. **Mileage** — when neither document provides one.

Everything else the lookups return, including the full MOT test history, is not case evidence the operator needs to read.

## Scope

- Remove the dedicated DVSA/DVLA section and the MOT history listing from the case page (`_CaseWorkflow.cshtml`, around the vehicle observation and estimated-mileage blocks).
- Keep the derived values where they belong — on the vehicle fields themselves, with their provenance, so the operator can still see that a value came from an external lookup rather than a document.
- Do **not** stop performing the lookups, and do not discard the observations: [[ENG-010]] proved MOT tests were being silently dropped, and the derived mileage depends on them. This is about what is displayed, not what is collected.

## How to verify

A case with a completed lookup shows no MOT history and no DVSA/DVLA section, while a vehicle detail or mileage that only the lookup could supply is still present and still labelled as externally sourced.
