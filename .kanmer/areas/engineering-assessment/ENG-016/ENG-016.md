## What

One act, not two. Export becomes the only way to produce the EVA package, and it
records the once-per-case `First sent to Engineer` proxy. The gated hand-off —
its route, its download route, its panel, its MCP surface and three of its four
tables — goes.

## Why

Operator direction (2026-08-24): *"so handoff and export are basically just
duplicated functions - they need to be combined"*, and *"yes include the first
send to engineer"*.

The duplication is real. Already shared: the package writer
(`EvaBundleSchema.CreateOfflineReplay`), image selection
(`EvaHandoffPolicy.SelectEligibleImages`), the evidence reader
(`BuildEvidence`), the image loader (`LoadEligibleImagesAsync`). Duplicated:
two mapping functions (`CaseEvaMapping.MapForProduction` /
`MapForOperatorExport`), two orchestration methods in `EvaHandoffStore`, two
routes, two Razor surfaces, two filename shapes.

After [[ENG-014]] the two produce a **byte-identical package**. The only real
differences left are the evidence bar and the recording — and the recording is
the half worth keeping.

The hand-off has never been used: it is switched off in production
(`docs/operations.md:571-573`), and `EvaHandoffRevisions`,
`EvaHandoffDownloadOperations` are empty (`:410-411`). The operator's actual
workflow is `CAP-010` — *"Extract JSON from the logged case and download it with
stored images for drag-and-drop into EVA"*. That is Export.

## The consequence to build, not to work around

**Export must become a `POST`.** It is currently
`GET /Cases/{caseId}/Documents/Export`, reached from a plain anchor
(`Details.cshtml:122-129`). A GET that records a business event is a genuine
hazard — browser prefetch and a plain refresh would both fire it, and it carries
no antiforgery token. Recording the proxy makes Export a write, so the control
becomes a form post, and the handler needs the idempotency the hand-off already
had: recording the proxy **once per case**, on first success only.

Reuse the existing shape rather than inventing one: `EvaHandoffPolicy.DecideRevision`
already encodes "first success records the proxy, later ones do not", and
`LocalEvaHandoffProxy` / `IEvaHandoffProxy` already refuse to claim delivery or
Engineer assignment (`CK_EvaFirstHandoffProxies_NoDeliveryClaim`).

## Scope

**Delete:**
- `src/Pegasus.Web/Pages/Cases/Eva/Download.cshtml` + `.cs`
- the `GenerateEvaHandoff` handler on the Vehicle page, and the EVA panel
  (`_CaseWorkflow.cshtml:384-391`)
- `IGenerateEvaHandoff`, `IDownloadEvaHandoff`, `GenerateEvaHandoff`,
  `DownloadEvaHandoff`, `IEvaHandoffQueries` and their DI registrations
  (`DependencyInjection.cs:395-402`)
- the hand-off MCP surface in `src/Pegasus.Web/Mcp/AssessmentMcpTools.cs`
- tables `EvaHandoffRevisions`, `EvaHandoffDownloadOperations`,
  `EvaHandoffOperations`, their entities and configuration
- `tests/Pegasus.IntegrationTests/EvaHandoffPersistenceTests.cs`

**Keep, and rewire:**
- `EvaFirstHandoffProxies` — it feeds the dashboard's "sent to engineer this
  week" count (`EfDashboardQueries.cs:78`). It currently carries a **required FK
  to `EvaHandoffRevisions`** (`EvaHandoffModelConfiguration.cs:76`); that FK must
  go with the table it points at.
- `IEvaHandoffProxy` / `LocalEvaHandoffProxy` — the no-delivery-claim guarantee.
- `EvaBundleSchema`, `EvaHandoffPolicy.SelectEligibleImages`, `BuildEvidence`.

**Merge:** the two mapping functions into one, and the two orchestration methods
into one.

## Assumption to confirm cheaply

**The surviving evidence bar is the permissive one.** `MapForProduction` refuses
unless all thirteen fields carry accepted, provenanced, non-empty evidence;
`MapForOperatorExport` ships what the case has. Keeping one act means keeping one
bar, and the operator already decided for Export ([[CASE-019]], 2026-08-22):
*"A blank field does not block the download."*

So `MapForProduction`'s fail-closed guard is **deleted**, not merged. Stated
prominently because it is the one genuinely consequential loss here — a case
with gaps will now be recordable as sent to an engineer. It does not touch the
`Fail closed before case creation` product invariant, which is about allocation,
not export. Reverse cheaply if that reading is wrong.

## Sequencing
