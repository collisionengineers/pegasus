## What

Add the fields the mockup records to `AssessmentVocabulary`: a damage list (zone, severity, type, note), tyres and seat belts, unrelated damage and deduction, material transfer, vehicle extras (VIN check, transmission, colour, body, tax and MOT expiry, airbags, fault codes, temporary repairs), settlement figures (excess, betterment, claimant VAT, reserve, hire, storage, recovery, diminution, salvage logistics, repair delays), fee lines; derive `impact_location` and `impact_severity`; project them into the report and the MCP update tool.

## Why

D39, D41. The vocabulary stores one impact location and no settlement detail. Mockup source: `Pegasus_UI_v2_src/src/04-fixtures.js` case shape; `Pegasus_UI_v2_notes.md` §Backend gaps 1–2.

## Approach

- One owner: `Core/Assessment/AssessmentContracts.cs`; projection in `Core/Reports/AssessmentReportProjection.cs`; Scriban template.
- Field storage stays the existing field map unless research proves a table is needed.
- AUTO-015 (MCP overwrite behaviour) is a linked prerequisite for the MCP side.
