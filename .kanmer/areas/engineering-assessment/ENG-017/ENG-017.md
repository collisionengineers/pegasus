## Why

Two rival definitions of "this image is a vehicle photograph" exist, and they
disagree on media type:

- `InstructionEvidenceImages.IsImage` accepts any `image/*`
  (`src/Pegasus.Core/Intake/InstructionEvidenceImages.cs`).
- `EvaHandoffPolicy.SelectEligibleImages` accepts only `image/jpeg` or
  `image/png` (`src/Pegasus.Core/Eva/EvaBundleSchema.cs:469`).

So a case whose only photograph is a `image/gif`, `image/webp` or `image/tiff`
attachment reads as images-complete, passes into Review, and is then refused at
export for having no stored vehicle image.

## Not a CASE-021 regression — checked, not assumed

Raised by automated review on [[CASE-021]] (PR #528). CASE-021 changed
`ImagesComplete` from a hardcoded `true` to an observed value, which *narrows*
this gap rather than widening it: before, a case with **no** images at all also
reached Review and failed at export. The media-type disagreement predates it.

Production was queried read-only while dispositioning that PR: all five intake
receipts carry `image/jpeg` attachments, so no live case is affected today.

## What to decide

Whether the media-type list is one owner consumed by both, or whether intake
completeness deliberately stays broader than EVA eligibility (they answer
different questions over different types — `IntakeAssetRecord` pre-custody
versus `EvaHandoffImageCandidate` post-custody). "One list per concept" in
AGENTS.md points at the first; the layering points at the second. Decide before
building.

## Verify

A case whose only photograph is a non-JPEG/PNG image reaches the same answer at
Review and at export, whichever answer is chosen.
