## What

`src/Pegasus.Infrastructure/Persistence/EvaHandoffStore.cs` (`FromVehicleField`, ~line 822) decides the EVA evidence status with:

```csharp
field.SourceKind.Equals("staff-correction", StringComparison.Ordinal)
    ? EvaEvidenceStatus.Corrected
    : EvaEvidenceStatus.Accepted
```

but the store writes the persisted source kind as `'staff_correction'` (underscore — `CaseDataCodes.StaffCorrection`, written by `EfVehicleWorkflowStore` on a Correct decision). The hyphenated literal never matches, so a staff-corrected vehicle field is always emitted into the EVA bundle as `Accepted`, never `Corrected`.

## Why

Found during TICK-021 ([[TICK-021]]) research (2026-08-20). Behaviour-affecting: the deterministic EVA bundle understates that a vehicle value was an explicit staff correction.

## Approach

Replace the literal with the persisted code (one list per concept — compare against the same constant the writer uses), and pin with a test that a corrected vehicle field yields `EvaEvidenceStatus.Corrected` in the bundle projection. Note: the fix changes deterministic bundle content for affected cases — confirm revision semantics (a regenerated hand-off is a new revision, which is the designed behaviour).

## Verification

- [ ] Test: staff-corrected vehicle field → `Corrected` status in the EVA evidence value.
- [ ] No other hyphen/underscore literal mismatches against `CaseDataCodes` (grep).
