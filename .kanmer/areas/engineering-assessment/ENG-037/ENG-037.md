## What

`AssessmentPolicy.NormalizeValue` (`src/Pegasus.Core/Assessment/AssessmentPolicy.cs`,
`case AssessmentFieldType.Date`) and `AssessmentReportProjection.ParseDate`
(`src/Pegasus.Core/Reports/AssessmentReportProjection.cs`) both call the
two-argument `DateOnly.TryParseExact(value, "yyyy-MM-dd", out var date)`
overload, which resolves against `CultureInfo.CurrentCulture`. Under a
non-Gregorian default calendar (for example `th-TH`, Thai Buddhist), the
canonical `yyyy-MM-dd` value `2027-01-02` parses to the Gregorian year 1484
and normalizes back out as a different date.

Switch both call sites to the `CultureInfo.InvariantCulture` /
`DateTimeStyles.None` overload and add a regression test that runs the
normalizer and the projection under a non-Gregorian culture.

## Why

Deferred from the ENG-035 review (PR #648, finding 2). The defect is
pre-existing on `dev` and was not introduced by ENG-035; ENG-035 added four
further `Date` vocabulary paths (`vehicle.tax_expiry`, `vehicle.mot_expiry`,
`settlement.hire_start`, `settlement.salvage.settled`) that inherit it, which
widens the blast radius but does not change the root cause.

## Verification

- [ ] Both call sites use the invariant-culture overload.
- [ ] A test asserts correct normalization and projection under `th-TH`.
