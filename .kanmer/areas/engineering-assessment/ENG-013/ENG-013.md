## Why — operator direction (2026-08-22, QDOS26011)

> "What seems to be happening here, based on another case, is that we are storing/showing BOTH the extracted details, AND the DVLA+DVSA lookup. The lookup is meant to be additive/enrichment. As in, if there are fields or information we don't have, this supplements our information."

> "From case QDOS26010 you can see this, as the case has two different mileages. An extracted, and our calculated version."

## Verified state (prod, read-only, 2026-08-22)

QDOS26011 `CaseDataFields` holds no `vehicle_mileage` row of any kind, so the Vehicle block reads "Not recorded".

Its `VehicleLookupObservations` row holds `MileageValue 121823`, `MileageUnit Miles`, `MileageObservedOn 2025-09-25`, `MileageMethodKey latest-mot-observation`, plus `ManufactureYear 2016` and `FuelType DIESEL`.

The two never meet. `EfVehicleWorkflowStore.AcceptAsync` writes lookup values into `CaseDataFields` **only** when staff press "Accept vehicle suggestion". Until then the observation is displayed beside the case fields as a rival, which is what reads as two mileages.

## The shape this should take

`CaseField<T>.Current => Confirmed ?? Fact ?? Suggestion` already models exactly this: a lookup value written as a **Suggestion** shows when nothing better exists and is silently outranked by an extracted `Fact` or a staff `Confirmed` value. No new concept is needed.

So: when a lookup observation is recorded, write its values as `Suggestion` rows with `SourceKind = vehicle_lookup`. Never overwrite a `Fact` or `Confirmed` row, and never remove one.

Manual acceptance stays exactly as it is — it promotes a Suggestion to Confirmed and remains the only way a lookup value becomes accepted evidence.

## Scope

- Write make, model, manufacture year, fuel type, mileage and mileage unit as Suggestions when the observation lands.
- Do not touch `Fact`/`Confirmed` precedence, `VehicleMileageEvidenceClassification`, or the accept/correct commands.
- The mileage keeps its derived-estimate classification word wherever it is shown ([[ENG-010]]).

## How to verify

QDOS26011's Vehicle block shows 121,823 Miles labelled as an external estimate rather than "Not recorded", while its extracted MAZDA make (a `Fact`) is unchanged and the lookup's own MAZDA does not appear twice.
