## What

Wave 4 of [[EPIC-011]]. Give the report-required assessment fields a real
operator writer through `ISaveAssessment`. `AssessmentPolicy` (`RequireField`,
`src/Pegasus.Core/Assessment/AssessmentPolicy.cs:200-222`) makes these
outstanding for the report draft, and `AssessmentReportRendering.cs:232`
makes `ImpactLocation` a required report source:

Vehicle type · Vehicle year · Mileage source · Pre-incident condition ·
Assessed date · Impact severity · **Impact location** · Retail value ·
Trade value · Engineer's value · Repairer VAT answer · Assessment outcome ·
Roadworthiness · Vehicle history check · Engineer name · Engineer
qualifications · Signature · Agreed fee.

Some already have a contract home elsewhere and belong to their owner, not
here: vehicle year / mileage source / history check sit in §1.8 Vehicle, and
the three valuations in §1.8 Valuations ([[CASE-029]]). This ticket owns the
remainder, which §1 draws nowhere: impact location, impact severity, assessed
date, vehicle type, pre-incident condition, repairer VAT answer, outcome,
roadworthiness, engineer name/qualifications/signature, agreed fee. Its first
job is to decide, with the operator, which surface draws them — the contract
must be extended before the markup is written.

## Why (regression, not new scope)

[[ENG-025]] ported `Pages/Cases/Assessment/**` to the §1.9 contract. On
`origin/dev` twelve of these fields were rendered outside any `<form>` behind
a `<button type="button">Save vehicle</button>` / `"Save incident and
impact"` — inert controls the §1.9 port was required to delete, so their
removal is the brief, not a loss.

**Impact location is different.** It had a real caller: the damage grid
(`Index.cshtml:1117-1131`, `asp-page-handler="SaveDamage"`, one submit button
per region) posting to `OnPostSaveDamageAsync`, shipped by [[ENG-006]] and
deployed to production. §1.9 draws no damage diagram and §1.14 removes the
section tabs the grid lived in, so ENG-025 deleted it with the section. Until
this ticket ships, impact location is settable only by Claude through
`src/Pegasus.Web/Mcp/AssessmentMcpTools.cs` — `ISaveAssessment` has no Razor
Pages caller at all.

## Owns

`src/Pegasus.Web/Pages/Cases/Assessment/**` (sequence with [[ENG-028]] — same
files, one at a time), assessment web tests. A contract amendment to
EPIC-011 `context.md` §1.9 if the operator puts the editor on the Assessment
page.

## Blocked by

[[ENG-025]] (the shell), and the operator decision on which surface draws
these fields.
