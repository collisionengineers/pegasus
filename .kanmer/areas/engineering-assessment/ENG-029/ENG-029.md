## What

Re-scoped 2026-09-02 for [[EPIC-012]] (D30, D41). The operator decided where the report-required fields are drawn: on the Case page, in the Settlement and Report sections that the sections-move ticket creates. This ticket gives them a real writer through `ISaveAssessment`:

- **Settlement** (`_CaseSettlement.cshtml`): outcome (Total loss / Repairable / Cash in lieu / Contract repair), category, salvage value, excess, betterment, claimant VAT registered, reserve, repair duration and delays, report delay, storage per day, recovery, hire start and daily cost, diminution, salvage logistics; derived repair cost, equity and ratio lines from the Current estimate and the Engineer's value.
- **Report** (`_CaseReport.cshtml`): Engineer's comments, vehicle history check, signing (sign-off) Engineer from the flagged accounts, agreed fee and description lines, readiness list (named outstanding items, never a percentage), Generate / Preview report draft.

Vehicle-type, condition, assessed date and roadworthiness are drawn in the Vehicle section (frame + CASE-029); impact location and severity are derived from the damage map ticket.

## Why (regression, not new scope)

The ENG-025 port deleted the inert field controls and the ENG-006 damage grid; `ISaveAssessment` has had no Razor Pages caller since. Mockup source: `Pegasus_UI_v2_src/src/22-case-engineer.js` §settlement and §report.
