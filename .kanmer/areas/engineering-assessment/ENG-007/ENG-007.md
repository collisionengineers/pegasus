## Why

The Assessment route calls IGetCase but independently queries vehicle evidence and implements a different Web-layer precedence rule. That duplication skips extracted vehicle facts and permits lookup observations to prefill over accepted instruction evidence.

## Acceptance

Assessment retains its route and assessment-owned query, but shared case and vehicle data come only through IGetCase. Prefill order is saved assessment, confirmed case value, extracted fact, then lookup observation; mileage value/unit/source remain consistent and online_data is used only for lookup-derived mileage.
