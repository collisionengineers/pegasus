## What

`AssessmentPolicy.EvaluatePostReviewReadiness`
(`src/Pegasus.Core/Assessment/AssessmentPolicy.cs:250-255`) still requires the
D18-era assessment fields `AssessmentVocabulary.EngineerName`,
`EngineerQualifications` and `EngineerSignature` as "Report content" readiness
items. `AssessmentReportProjection.Prepare` seeds its reasons from that rail,
so a complete D31 sign-off tuple alone does not make a report ready, and a
sign-off Engineer with no qualifications (Neil, D31) still depends on the
assessment record's own `engineer.qualifications` field being populated.

## Why

D31 makes the rendered signatory the Case's sign-off Engineer account tuple,
not the typed assessment identity. [[DOCS-017]] removed the projection's reads
of those fields and its accepted-signatory allowlist but was explicitly scoped
away from `AssessmentContracts.cs` and `AssessmentPolicy.cs`. Raised as
blocker 1 by the DOCS-017 PR review (PR #651, gpt-5.6-terra xhigh, 2026-09-03)
and deferred here.

## Approach

- Inventory every consumer of `AssessmentVocabulary.Engineer*` (assessment
  form, MCP update tool, Review-entry readiness, report readiness) before
  removing anything.
- Remove them from post-Review report readiness so the D31 tuple is the only
  signatory requirement; decide separately whether the fields survive at all.
- Land after [[CASE-040]] and [[PLAT-068]] supply the tuple, so readiness is
  never left with no signatory requirement at all.

## Verification

- [ ] A case with a complete D31 sign-off tuple and no `engineer.*` assessment
      fields reports no signatory readiness gap and generates a report.
- [ ] A sign-off Engineer with no qualifications renders the name alone
      end-to-end.
- [ ] No remaining Core consumer of the retired vocabulary paths.
