## What

Add case-scoped, human-controlled preparation of report photographs: non-destructive crops, a deterministic selected order, a distinct required Close-up in position one, a distinct required Overview in position two, and optional supporting images after them.

## Why

Pegasus currently offers every custody-confirmed Case image to the report in occurrence order. The supplied work-pack requirement activates the UI-15 curation seam without changing source custody or reviving a standalone Images area.

## Approach

- Reuse the authorised CASE-006/DOCS-011 viewer and CASE-027 evidence surface.
- Store versioned selection, normalized crop geometry, role and order in the existing SQL/application boundary; never replace the source bytes or add a permanent derivative Blob store.
- Restrict eligibility to current custody-confirmed Case-vehicle evidence and preserve existing third-party/reflection exclusions.
- Snapshot curation into each generated report version; later changes produce a later report version.
- Keep the work in the Case/Assessment surfaces and use labels/actions without explanatory UI copy.
