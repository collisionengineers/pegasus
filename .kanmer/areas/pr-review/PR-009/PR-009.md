## What

Fix the integrated renderer so long accepted repair lists and multiple photos flow to the later Statement of Truth/signature section without omission, while retaining normal rendererref1 styling and page furniture.

## Why

TICK-213's required real-Chromium stress proof exposed a production defect on merged `dev`. A Repairable snapshot with 80 uniquely labelled new parts, 80 repairs, 80 operations and 8 accepted hashed photos produced a multi-page PDF containing the terminal `080` entries but the extracted PDF omitted the later `Statement of Truth` section. The existing representative one-item/one-photo test passes, so it does not cover this boundary.

## Approach

- Reproduce with the verification-only Browser test currently prepared in TICK-213's worktree.
- Determine whether Chromium pagination, the photo grid, or explicit page-break interaction omits the trailing section.
- Apply the smallest renderer/template/CSS correction under FRD-11; do not add caller-selectable density or global auto-fit.
- Prove all terminal work-list entries, all accepted photos, Statement of Truth/signature, and per-page reference/page furniture survive a real Chromium render.
