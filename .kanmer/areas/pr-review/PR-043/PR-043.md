## Why

At PR #477 head `fc3b651e`, an operation-key replay enters `RecoverAsync` for both `pending` and `uncertain`. A duplicate POST arriving while the original provider call is still in flight can probe the unchanged source, mark the shared operation `failed`, and release the filtered active-claim index. A new-key retry may then be admitted while the original call later succeeds, allowing a duplicate external move despite PR-038.

## Verify

- Treat a genuinely in-flight `pending` operation as still active; do not infer failure merely because a concurrent probe still sees its source.
- Define the smallest durable transition that makes a provider result eligible for uncertain recovery, without adding a worker/framework or blind retry.
- Prove overlapping same-key POSTs cannot free the active slot, cannot issue a second provider move, and cannot admit a new-key retry until the original operation reaches a terminal or recoverable uncertain state.
- Preserve the existing same-key status check for genuinely uncertain operations and different-key filtered serialization.
