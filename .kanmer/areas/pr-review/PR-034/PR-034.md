## What

Keep explicitly attached Content-ID images in the canonical attachment ordinal sequence.

## Why

At PR #469 head `7932d683`, the intake reader treats any image with a Content-ID as inline even when `Content-Disposition` says attachment. MimeKit retains that part in the displayed attachment list, but the canonical descriptor list omits it, shifting every later searchable attachment ordinal and potentially attributing searchability/matches to the wrong row. This blocks [[TICK-053]].

## Verification

- Prove an explicitly attached Content-ID image followed by a searchable document has identical canonical/display ordinals.
- Exclude only genuinely inline images; preserve the single reader/projection/store.
