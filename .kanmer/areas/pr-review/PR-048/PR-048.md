## Why

The new Mail POST handlers perform fresh receipt/Case checks and acquire a lease before calling the replay-protected Core association command. Repeating the exact successful link or unlink POST after a lost response is therefore rejected as stale/current-state conflict before the canonical same-key replay path is reached.

## Required outcome

Keep the exact Mail link and unlink submissions replay-safe without adding a second association policy or generic action framework. A same-key replay must report the already-applied result, create no new mutation/history, and remain distinct from a conflicting key or changed inputs.

## Verification

- [ ] Repeat the exact successful Mail link POST and prove success with one link history entry.
- [ ] Repeat the exact successful Mail unlink POST and prove success with one unlink history entry.
- [ ] Prove changed inputs under the same operation identity fail closed.
- [ ] Preserve the existing `ILinkIntake` / `IReverseIntakeLink` and EF transaction as the only business owner.
