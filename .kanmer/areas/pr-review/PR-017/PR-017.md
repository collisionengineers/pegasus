## What

Populate Deleted Items mailbox refinements from the approved mailbox estate rather than only retained Inbox rows.

## Why

`ListMailboxesAsync` derives tabs from `RetainedMailboxMessages`. An approved mailbox with no retained Inbox row cannot be selected for exact Deleted Items search even though the Graph source accepts it, so the required mailbox-specific refinement is absent. This blocks [[TICK-053]].

## Verification

- Prove a pollable approved mailbox with zero retained rows appears as a selectable Deleted Items scope.
- Do not invent retained Inbox history or duplicate the approved-estate owner.
