## Why

The new Mail handlers acquire a five-minute Case edit lease immediately before link/unlink. If the association call then fails (for example a concurrent receipt change or eligibility failure), the recoverable-error path reloads the page but does not release or otherwise resolve the acquired lease. The next confirmation uses a new operation key and can be blocked until expiry despite the UI telling staff to retry.

## Required outcome

Use the existing lease ports and recovery conventions so a definitively failed Mail association does not strand edit authority. Preserve uncertain-outcome safety and do not introduce a Mail-specific lease store or transaction framework.

## Verification

- [ ] Force a failure after lease acquisition and prove the Case is immediately editable/retryable under the accepted recovery rule.
- [ ] Prove successful link/unlink still consumes/clears the lease through the canonical mutation.
- [ ] Prove no duplicate association or history is created.
