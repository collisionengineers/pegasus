# Research — TICK-205: canonical repair specification and dual Audit specifications

## Question

How should Pegasus reconcile ENG-01's one canonical repair specification with the operator-accepted RPT-03 requirement to retain conservative and maximised Audit specifications and compute their uplift?

## Findings

- The operator resolved the product choice on 2026-08-19: an Audit retains **two immutable repair specifications**, labelled conservative and maximised, and computes their uplift. Source: resolved SIMPLI-014 `open-questions` (“all yes”), item for [[TICK-205]].
- ENG-01 is a source/provenance rule, not a prohibition on an Audit comparison pair. It allocates one canonical repair specification with route provenance for Glass's, Audatex PDF, or an approved AI proposal; every route still requires accepted source, mapping, caller and Engineer review. Sources: `docs/capabilities.md` ENG-01; [[TICK-093]]; FRD-06 professional-engineering authority.
- RPT-03 is the Audit-specific downstream behaviour: “Audit rendering preserves conservative and maximised specifications and records their uplift,” and both accepted specification versions are required. Source: `docs/capabilities.md` RPT-03.
- The consistent model is therefore: a normal assessment/version has one accepted canonical repair specification; an Audit report has two distinct, role-labelled accepted specification versions—`conservative` and `maximised`—each canonical for its role. Neither overwrites or aliases the other. The comparison is intentional Audit evidence rather than duplicate business implementation.
- FRD-06 already requires accepted professional facts to remain Core/authorised-human owned, and corrections to create a reasoned superseding finding/version with actor, time, source, structured before/after values and the prior version retained. That rule applies independently to each Audit specification role. Source: `docs/frd/frd-06-vehicle-and-engineering-evidence.md` § Professional engineering findings and correction.
- FRD-11 already requires deterministic template/payload versioning, preserved provenance, authorised human review, immutable issued artifact identity/hash and correction/addendum rather than overwrite. It does not yet spell out the dual-specification Audit input or uplift calculation. Source: `docs/frd/frd-11-reports-correspondence-and-reviewed-proposals.md`.
- Current Core assessment data cannot represent the accepted Audit model. `CaseAssessmentProjection.EstimateLines` is one ordered collection, and `SaveAssessmentRequest.EstimateLines` optionally replaces that whole collection. Lines carry type, description, work units, price, status, evidence label and confirmation provenance, but have no specification id, role, version, source-route identity, accepted total or supersession link. Source: `src/Pegasus.Core/Assessment/AssessmentContracts.cs`.
- Current persistence likewise stores one case-owned estimate-line collection and its history around assessment saves. It does not expose two simultaneously current role-labelled Audit specification records. Sources: `src/Pegasus.Infrastructure/Persistence/AssessmentEntities.cs`, `AssessmentModelConfiguration.cs`, `EfCaseAssessmentStore.cs`.
- The existing `AuditAssessment` enum (`Repairable`/`TotalLoss`) and Audit reference/custody types concern case allocation identity and the original-report evidence. They do not model the conservative/maximised specification pair and must not be overloaded for it. Sources: `src/Pegasus.Core/Cases/CaseContracts.cs`; `Custody/CustodyContracts.cs`.
- The renderer workspace has no Audit template or Audit-specific model. Its catalogue has assessment/general report families, and searches find no conservative/maximised specification or uplift contract. `rendererref1` also describes assessment reports with one worklist, not Audit rendering. Sources: `workspaces/report-renderer/src/CollisionRenderer.Core/TemplateCatalog.cs`, `Models/Documents.cs`; `reference/rendererref1/report_data_schema.json`; resolved [[TICK-207]].
- The operator separately deferred Audit rendering until a representative Audit report/template is supplied or approved; assessment samples must not be used to invent Audit wording. This ticket can settle the data/behaviour contract but cannot claim RPT-03 rendering acceptance. Source: resolved SIMPLI-014 `open-questions`; [[TICK-207]].
- “Uplift” must be a computed result, never independently entered or stored as another policy value. Consistent with RPT-01's compute-once rule, Core should derive the monetary difference from the two accepted specification totals. A percentage, if product-visible later, must also be derived using one specified denominator and rounding rule; no accepted source currently defines that percentage contract. Sources: RPT-01/RPT-03 capability wording; AGENTS.md one-list/one-owner rules.
- Each specification needs its own immutable identity/version, role, ordered lines, source route and source artifact/version, actor/review/acceptance time, calculation-rule version, totals and supersession lineage. A report payload binds exact accepted versions of both; later correction creates a new version for the affected role and a new report/addendum rather than rewriting either historical specification or report. Sources: FRD-06 correction; FRD-11 report finality; EPIC-004 context.
- The Audit comparison must fail closed unless exactly one accepted current conservative version and one accepted current maximised version belong to the same Audit case/reference and compatible calculation/rate/VAT basis. Missing, ambiguous, unconfirmed or cross-case inputs cannot produce uplift or render. Sources: repository fail-closed invariants; RPT-03 requires both accepted versions; EPIC-004 context.
- The original third-party report/source remains separate custody evidence. The two Collision Engineers specifications may be derived/reviewed from it, but source artifact identity and mapping provenance must be retained rather than flattening it into estimate lines. Sources: operator-notes Audit meaning; INT-25/standalone Audit evidence; FRD-06 source-labelled evidence requirements.

## Implications

- Update the governing behaviour so ENG-01 says “one accepted canonical specification per purpose/version,” with Audit explicitly owning two required roles. RPT-03/FRD-11 then consume those exact accepted role versions and derive uplift.
- Do not add a boolean such as `IsMaximised` to the current singleton line collection. The persistent aggregate needs an explicit specification identity and closed role vocabulary so both records coexist, version and correct independently.
- Reuse the current estimate-line vocabulary and confirmation/provenance conventions where they fit, but move line ownership under a versioned repair-specification aggregate rather than maintaining a second Audit-only line implementation.
- Core owns role, acceptance, compatibility, total/uplift calculation, fail-closed selection and correction lineage. Infrastructure persists those decisions; the renderer receives already accepted, exact-version data and must not choose specifications or calculate business uplift independently.
- RPT-03 implementation remains blocked on [[TICK-207]]'s representative Audit template even after this contract is documented and implemented.
- No new operator question remains for TICK-205: the choice was explicitly accepted. The technical plan must define the monetary uplift formula and either exclude percentage uplift or obtain a separately accepted denominator/rounding rule before exposing it.

## Open questions

- None requiring operator input for this ticket. The dual immutable specification model and uplift direction are accepted.
- Audit layout/wording remains explicitly deferred to [[TICK-207]], not silently assumed here.
