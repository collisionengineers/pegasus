Current tree:

1. Received

- General [standard e-mail types that can apply to any category e.g. autoreply, undeliverable, acknowledgements such as "thank you" ]

- billing [payment notifications, remittances, invoice requests, billing-query, general-billing]

- new-work-received [where we receive initial work instructions. subcategories of types e.g. audit, diminution, inspection, new-client, website-work]

- non-client-related [any emails internal to the company e.g. from the tools / services we use, software packages etc]

- in-progress-cases [any emails related to an ongoing case such as: cancellation, case-update, client-chasing-for-update, etc]

- post-report-emails [emails received after we have sent a report to the client. queries, disputes, amendment requests, and similar]

- pre-instruction-emails [e-mails received regarding a case before we've actually been sent the case. this includes triage requests, general requests for us to handle a case in a certain way before we receive it, and images being received before we actually get the formal instructions]

- internal-cc

2. Sent


- Report sent [Our e-mail sending the engineers report]
- case-rejected [Where we may reject a case for whatever reason]
- query-sent [where we have additional queries/information we need]
- additional-image-request [where we have images, but they were not sufficient and we need better ones]

Use the outlook connector to view e-mails. 

3. Reply - not technically a recorded type as the e-mails will fall into a 1 or 2 category

- Our replies to any of the e-mails from 1. Mirror received category but with [reply ]
- Their replies to any of the e-mails from 2. Mirror received but with [reply ]
