# Diminution and Commercial cases

> **Archive status — non-authoritative planning evidence.** Revalidate against current product, roadmap, architecture, operations, design, decisions, and code before use.

Primary plan: `P-LATERCASE`

Features: `CASE-05`; `CASE-06` — `Later`/`unallocated` release work.
Pre-conversion status: **Planned decision/contract activation; no case-type policy or caller is implemented.**

## Shared boundary

These are separate case-type slices over the existing case identity, immutable principal/reference, lifecycle, custody and action-history boundaries. Core owns each approved case-type policy; an authenticated Web workspace is the intended caller after the `Later`/`unallocated` UI route. No copied intake, allocation, lifecycle, party/CRM or commercial engine is implied.

## Add Diminution cases

Feature: `CASE-05` — `Later`/`unallocated` release work.

Before implementation, obtain a direct business decision defining eligibility, source/claimant evidence, actor permissions, case fields, lifecycle transitions, valuation/repairer/EVA interaction, closure, correction, report and finance relationship. The slice rejects missing/contradictory evidence, unauthorised actor, unsupported transition, stale edit or uncertain principal before allocation/change; it never changes a principal/reference after allocation. Its contract must say whether it reuses or requires an approved extension to existing identity/lifecycle data.

Core negative tests, migration/concurrency proof where approved, and an intended authenticated caller journey must precede a limited operator evaluation. A file, mockup, or direct database test is not caller evidence. Roll out only after product, UI, data/privacy and operator/release approval; recover by withdrawing the new caller and preserving cases/action history for reasoned correction, never deletion. Excluded now are a Diminution formula, vendor valuation, finance workflow, external API, schema or migration until the decision identifies them. Stable case/principal/reference/source identities remain the seam; any case-type data migration is an irreversible choice requiring explicit approval and recovery evidence.

## Add Commercial cases

Feature: `CASE-06` — `Later`/`unallocated` release work.

This independently needs a direct business contract for eligibility, commercial parties/authority, documents, workflow, reporting, fees/finance relationship, correction, closure and privacy/retention implications. It has the same Core and intended Web caller boundary, but may not borrow Diminution policy merely because both are `Later`/`unallocated`. Fail closed on incomplete or ambiguous commercial authority, identity, transition or data; retain reviewable source evidence and allocate nothing automatically.

Proof, rollout and recovery mirror the Diminution slice but require a commercial-specific representative, non-corpus operator journey. Accounting, invoicing, estimating, valuation, external systems and new parties/accounts are excluded until separately activated. Future activation evidence is the accepted contract, named data model/migration decision where needed, caller-backed negative and operator evidence, and exact security/cost approval.

## Deferred-capability impact

`CASE-05` and `CASE-06` are activation plans, not settled business-policy specifications. They preserve existing identity/lifecycle seams and explicitly avoid dormant case types, routes, flags, adapters or storage. Their later finance/EVA dependencies stay independently gated.
