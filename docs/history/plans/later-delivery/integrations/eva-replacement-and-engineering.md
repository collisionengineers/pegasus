## Programme boundary

Current V1 work exports a successful EVA JSON/image bundle and records the CollisionSpike `Sent to Engineer` proxy; EVA retains actual named-Engineer assignment. Replacement cannot be inferred from that export. Each stage needs a direct product contract, named Core owner/intended caller, data/migration/adapter boundary, vendor/security/licence/cost approval, caller-backed evidence and operator/release acceptance. No current EVA API, replacement, engineering, estimating, valuation, reporting or AI-Assessor caller exists.

## Independent activation slices

### Activate direct EVA API

Feature: `EXT-04` — V3+ release work.

This requires evidence that EVA offers a usable approved API, exact endpoint/data/identity scope, failure/retry/idempotency, retention and recovery agreement. An integration adapter is an intended composition-root caller of existing named Core use cases; it cannot become the case/lifecycle owner. Refuse unapproved scope, ambiguous correlation or uncertain side effect before a vendor call. A single exact approved non-production/shared-development smoke proves only that scope.

### Replace EVA engineering workflow

Features: `CASE-22`; `EXT-05`; `EXT-06`; `EXT-07`; `EXT-08` — V3+ release work.

Inspection/report-preparation replacement, Engineer assignment, estimating, valuation and report generation require separately accepted domain contracts even where their user journey is connected. The Core owner(s) must define authority, assignment eligibility, review/revision, evidence, lifecycle/report finality, correction and recovery before a Web/Worker caller is implemented. Missing/ambiguous evidence, unauthorised/stale actor, unsupported transition or failed external correlation fails closed with no silent assignment/report or alteration of case identity.

### Deliver estimating and valuation workflows

Features: `EXT-09`; `EXT-10` — V3+ release work.

Repair estimate and vehicle valuation are separate business contracts. They do not activate merely because EVA replacement does, and they do not settle a formula, data source, valuation policy, vendor, fee or finance rule now. They need their own Core policy, intended caller, model/data decision, correction/audit route and operator evaluation.

### Integrate approved estimating and valuation services

Features: `EXT-12`; `EXT-13` — V3+ release work.

Audatex/other estimating and valuation services each require a separately approved vendor contract and adapter. Enforce exact target, no-call scope denial, idempotency, typed permanent/transient/unknown outcomes and reconciliation. Vendor registration is not an activated caller.

### Offer staff-selected AI Assessor

Feature: `AI-07` — V3+ release work.

Only after accepted EVA-replacement assignment policy may staff select the `AI Assessor` Engineer option. This is distinct from AI assistance and needs a direct role/assignment/accountability, explanation, correction, privacy/licence/cost and evaluation decision. It cannot autonomously assign an engineer or replace staff selection.

## Evidence, recovery and deferral

Each slice progresses from Core negatives and approved migration/adapter tests to an intended-caller journey, then one exact external/shared-development scope and operator acceptance. Record permanent action history separately from content-safe telemetry. Roll out one stage/vendor/caller at a time; recover by disabling the approved integration/caller and reconciling durable attempts/evidence without deleting cases, reports or identities. Excluded now are all vendor accounts, APIs, schemas, migrations, queues, prompts, commercial formulas and finance capability. Stable case/source/report/external-correlation identities are preserved; any replacement-data migration or report-finality contract is an explicitly approved irreversible choice.
