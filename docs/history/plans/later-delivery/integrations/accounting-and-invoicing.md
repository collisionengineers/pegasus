## Deliver accounting and invoicing workflow

Accounting/invoicing is independent of EVA replacement even if it may later consume approved report/estimate/valuation evidence. Before implementation, obtain a direct finance/business decision for charge authority, invoice identity/numbering, tax, adjustments/credit/correction, approval/segregation, accounting system scope, retention, reconciliation, reporting, failure ownership and audit requirements. A finance Core policy owns accepted business decisions; an authenticated staff Web route or approved integration is only an intended caller.

Fail closed for absent/ambiguous charge authority, unauthorised actor, duplicate/stale request, unsupported correction, uncertain tax/recipient, or external reconciliation uncertainty. Do not create/alter a case reference or hide a financial outcome. Durable finance/audit history is separate from content-safe telemetry. Core negatives and approved accounting-contract tests precede caller-backed workflows; an exact vendor smoke needs separate identity, target, data, security, licence/cost and operator/finance approval. It proves neither accounting correctness beyond the agreed fixture nor production reconciliation.

Roll out per approved workflow/accounting target with idempotency and recovery evidence; recover by disabling the caller/integration and reconciling durable outcomes through the accepted correction process, never deletion. Excluded now: chart of accounts, tax logic, payment collection, ledger, accounting adapter, credentials, schema/migration and vendor selection. Preserve case/report/estimate/valuation and future invoice/external-correlation identities only once accepted; invoice numbering or posted financial records are irreversible choices requiring explicit contract and recovery approval.

## Deferred-capability impact

No EVA or case plan may silently absorb finance work. Future activation requires the accepted finance contract, named Core owner/caller, migration and correction/reconciliation route where needed, and independent finance/operator evidence.
