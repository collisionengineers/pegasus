# Motion

## Purpose and principles

No product-wide motion system is needed or exercised. The upstream marketing
scroll reveals, staggered entrances, hover scaling, and CTA lift are excluded
from this operational application. A basic non-essential refresh/loading animation is permitted; feedback must remain understandable without motion.

## Durations/easing/source

None. Do not invent duration/easing tokens during onboarding.

## Reduced motion

Planned `0.1.0-alpha.1` behavior respects reduced-motion preferences. Any later motion must
remain optional, restrained, and verified through the approved UI route. The
refresh/loading animation must have a static reduced-motion equivalent.
