# Case record: how it should work

Proposed on 9 October 2026; not yet decided. Each rule is one sentence the FRD can carry once the operator settles its lettered item in the [notes](../../current/v36-notes.md). Alignment-only findings (tier a) change no rule and are not listed here.

## Ribbon and section row

- D1. "{Name} is editing" is stated once, on the ribbon chip beside Take over; section heads carry no repeated label while a colleague holds the lease. (item A)
- D2. Below 980px only the section row stays sticky; the ribbon scrolls with the page. (item B)
- D3. The aside's AI draft row names the AI Proposal by its reserved term; "Estimate" stays where it names a source document or the target of the spec Send to AI creates. (item C)

## Aside

- D4. On a Held Case the Next action step reads the state with its review date and offers Release Hold as its control, not a link to Case details. (item D)

## Case details and Claim

- D5. Case details does not repeat the ribbon's Case type, reference and Principal as greyed cells. (item E)
- D6. Claimant VAT status (Case data, free text) and Claimant VAT registered (assessment, Yes/No) stay distinct facts side by side; whether they should be one is an open question for FRD-16. (item F, Open)

## Inspection details and Decisions

- D7. Storage per day and Recovery charge are Engineer figures recorded in Decisions' Costs, hire & delays; Inspection details' Storage keeps the location alone. (item G)
- D8. A select offers only the choices that can be chosen; an unavailable Inspect at choice is absent, not disabled with a suffix. (item H)
- D9. Decisions' metric strip goes; Figures is the home of Repair cost inc VAT, Engineer's Value and Repair cost of value, and Labour hours is a read-only cell beside Excess. (item I)
- D10. A recorded choice (Outcome, Roadworthiness, Salvage category) reads as the greyed value box like every other cell, and its segments are the edit control; "Not recorded" is the box's absent word, never a chosen segment. (item J, with the operator's choice of variant)

## Vehicle and Damage

- D11. "Experian is not connected" is stated once, on the Vehicle history sub-panel. (item K)
- D12. Damage has no Recorded areas count box; the recorded-areas list states what is recorded. (item L)

## Valuation

- D13. Get valuation sits where the operator chooses: a text link in the card head, the card's foot at full width, or inline with Guide month. (item M)
- D14. An unconnected guide card states the approved sentence as one quiet line under its figures, not a notice box. (item N)
- D15. Reading shows the Value increases sub-panel and the deductions in the same geometry as editing, greyed, so entering edit grows nothing. (item O)

## Report, Files and Notes

- D16. The Statement of truth is a sub-panel folded until opened, remembered by the fold cookie. (item P)
- D17. Add Case note is a secondary button; a screen shows one red primary. (item Q)
- D18. The Repair Spec origin line names the person, never an account id. (tier a, no rule change, noted for Stage 2)

## Behaviour (Stage 2 scope)

- D19. Navigating link buttons (Assign Engineer, Open Triage, Open full Case, Review source, the Next action control, Add evidence, Send chaser, Open report) show the established busy state while the next page loads. (item R)
- D20. A Workflow or Closure action returns the reader to the section it was taken from, with its notice. (item S)
- D21. A landed save redraws the ribbon, notices and aside once, re-anchors the reading line, and restores focus wherever it was; the first save of a session records only the fields that changed. (item T)
- D22. The Scroll/Tabs choice lasts as long as the fold choice; Tabs switching and section jumps move instantly, not by glide. (item U)
- D23. The Actions menu closes when an item opens a dialog; the crop toast sits above the viewer and no longer says "Save the Case". (item V)
- D24. A section head offers Edit only where the viewer can edit that section, and a locked section says why in one label. (item W)
- D25. The spec grid: figures and their headers right-aligned, two decimals, the part number left, and the money headers read "Unit (£)" and "Material (£)" or "Unit" and "Material" with a £ prefix in the cell. (item X, with the operator's choice of variant)

Open: whether Report keeps the five-paragraph Statement of truth on the page at all once the report prints it (D16 folds it; removing it is a Report-content decision).
Open: the empty value word across the page ("Not recorded", "—", "none", "Not applicable"); the walk lists them and proposes no change.
