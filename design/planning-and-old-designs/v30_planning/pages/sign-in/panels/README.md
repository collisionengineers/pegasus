# Sign in: panels

One card. Its parts, top to bottom: brand row (`auth-brand`), h1, validation
summary, form (`field` × 2, `btn btn--primary`).

## Three initial-login alternatives

A places the brand and form directly on one white surface. B separates
identity and form into charcoal and white panels, stacked below 980px.
C retains one white card on charcoal, with the brand centred above the
form. All three use the same heading, validation region and two fields.
See the [proposals](../../../current/signin-design-proposals.md).
