# Inbox: how it should work

Stage 1 proposals, **not yet decided**. The lettered items are in
[v30-notes.md](../../current/v30-notes.md#sign-off-list) (items L to P).

1. The sort toggle keeps its words, **Received**, and draws the
   `arrow-up-down` glyph instead of a Unicode arrow.
2. The filter row keeps its three labelled selects; the Search field is
   the wide cell and its button is attached to the field, so the row is one
   line at 1580 and 1440 and two at 1180.
3. A row's date and time keep a fixed right column; the excerpt is one
   line; attachments read as the `paperclip` glyph and the count; the meta
   line orders chip, classification, Case reference.
4. The row's Dismiss or Restore button is quiet until the row is hovered or
   focused. It is never hidden.
5. The preview pane keeps its facts and actions; the subject is the
   pane's first line, attachments are chips, and **Open full message**
   stays the dark action.
6. The Inbox empty state is one sentence, "No mail has been received.",
   and the retention explanation goes.

Open: L (sort glyph), M (search field and button), N (row meta and
attachments), O (quiet Dismiss), P (empty-state sentence).
