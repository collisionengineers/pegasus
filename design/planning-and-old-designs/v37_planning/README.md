# v37 planning: Management Reports

**Historical reference, retained by operator instruction on 9 October 2026 (item S).** This round was a mockup of Administration › Management Reports in three alternative designs, switched on the mockup strip, with proposals for the reports behind the page. The operator chose Design A with every lettered item settled, and Stage 2 implemented it. These mockups and planning notes preserve the design evidence; they are not application code, design authority or implementation evidence. Current behaviour is owned by [FRD-17](../../../docs/frd/frd-17-administration-workspace.md#management-reports) "Management Reports" and the PRDs, FRDs and accepted ADRs linked from [the documentation index](../../../docs/index.md).

- [current/](current/README.md): the offline mockups, their build, self-check and live-capture scripts, screenshots, the notes with the settled sign-off items, and the discussion log.
- [pages/](pages/README.md): the Management Reports page folder, with how it worked before the round and how it should work, as decided.
