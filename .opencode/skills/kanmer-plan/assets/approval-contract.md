# Approval contract — <outcome>

*Human approval surface. Aim for 300–600 words; this is guidance only and is never a Kanmer gate.*

## Outcome
One observable end state, not a solution narrative.
## Why
Why this outcome matters now.
## User or operational effect
Plain-language change and who notices it.
## In scope
Boundaries this approval authorizes.
## Out of scope
Explicit exclusions.
## Key decisions
Settled choices; resolve unknowns before implementation.
## Main risks
Material risks, mitigation/rollback, and owner.
## Breakdown
Ticket IDs, outcomes, and dependency order; not internal steps.
## Evidence
Checks, observation, or operational record that proves the outcome.
## Approval boundary
Approval authorizes <scope>; implementation, merge, and deployment remain separately controlled.
