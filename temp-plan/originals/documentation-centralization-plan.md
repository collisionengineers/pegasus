# Documentation centralization plan

## Decision

Replace the current multi-index, multi-status documentation graph with one small canonical spine, preserve active rationale plus immutable superseded decision/change bodies required by their own record contracts, executable/support documentation, legal notices beside retained dependencies, machine-readable contracts, and unique raw source evidence, and delete other historical/derived documents after their unique payload is moved. Do not create another archive, compatibility layer, status ledger, or documentation database.

This plan covers **512 in-scope artifacts** read across repository, design, reference, Azure, and three source workspaces. `docs/reference/imp-docs/**` and every descendant were completely excluded: no file was inspected, summarized, cited, moved, or used to derive a requirement. Repository instruction/context files supplied by the harness remain operational context rather than consolidation targets.

The 512 count follows the census contract below: Markdown plus documentation-bound JSON/YAML/DOCX, unique raw/historical reference artifacts, two packaged extractor schemas, and the three design-candidate rasters that this plan deletes. Other runtime assets are not counted.

The resulting footprint is **238 artifacts**: a reduction of **274 (53.5%)**. Most of the remaining volume is not prose sprawl: 102 AI Centre artifacts are predominantly self-contained executable source packages, with two superseded workspace ADRs retained as immutable provenance; 48 reference artifacts are unique raw schemas, samples, screenshots, workbooks, or still-needed source evidence. The human-maintained Pegasus documentation spine becomes eight obvious owners plus active and contractually retained superseded ADR/change records.

## Census contract

The audit base commit is `467284f23b268e199d7fbe77dbb2163b50f00e23`; the baseline is that commit plus the pre-consolidation working-tree state of the in-census artifacts. The appendix is the exact 512-path baseline. At implementation start, generate a temporary non-repository manifest from those rows containing relative path, byte length, SHA-256, base commit, and working-tree marker; attach the manifest plus its separately computed SHA-256/totals to exact-head review output, then discard the execution copy after acceptance. This makes the census reproducible without creating a repository status ledger.

The temporary disposition manifest is JSON with `schemaVersion: 1`, the exact `baseCommit`, `excludedPrefix: "docs/reference/imp-docs/"`, `baseline` (512 objects with `domain`, `path`, `action` in `K|R|M|D`, `targets`, and `noUniquePayload`), and `creates` (13 `domain`/`path` objects). `M` requires one new-path target; `D` requires at least one exact destination or `noUniquePayload: true`; K/R paths target themselves. The generated baseline/head manifests use `schemaVersion`, `baseCommit`, `artifacts` (`path`, `byteLength`, `sha256`, `workingTreeMarker`), and `excludedPathNames`. After writing each manifest, the script separately computes and prints that file's SHA-256; a self-hash is not embedded in the hashed JSON. `excludedPathNames` contains names only: the enumerator must not stat/read/hash those targets.

## Canonical end state

`docs/index.md` remains the only repository-wide map. Every question has one owner:

| Question | Canonical owner after consolidation |

| What does one active/reviewed or uniquely retained completed change own? | `docs/changes/README.md` plus one record per change |
| What is the durable UI rule/source-runtime map? | `design/README.md` plus three `design/product/*` contracts |

| What are exact Azure evidence/design/retirement routes? | `docs/azure/README.md` and required `.azure/deployment-plan.md` |
| What does an independently buildable source workspace own? | that workspace's root `README.md`, minimal live technical docs, machine contracts, active ADRs, and immutable superseded ADR provenance required by its local decision policy |

The rewritten `docs/index.md` preserves this exact precedence order; no surviving owner may restate or reorder it:

1. Current explicit user direction.
2. Declared active product/operator authority and controlled external requirements.
3. Accepted ADR/design authority within scope.
4. Current architecture and operations.
5. Code, configuration, tests, CI, IaC, and authorized live reads as current-state evidence.
6. Draft/discovery sources.
7. History/superseded material.

Rules added to `docs/index.md`:

1. A live claim is written once. Every other document links to its owner.
2. `Now`/version allocation, implemented, caller-proved, deployed, live-verified, and accepted remain distinct states.
3. Evergreen pages do not carry test counts, file counts, package versions, or live-resource state unless the value is itself the contract. Dated evidence carries a head/checkpoint and scope.
4. Historical-only prose outside immutable decision and uniquely reviewed change records is distilled then deleted. No `docs/history/` replacement is created.
5. Published decision bodies are never narrowed, rewritten, or deleted in place. Current/superseded status and forward links live in the decision index; changed decisions use accepted addenda or new superseding records.
6. Source workspaces keep only current technical contracts, immutable ADRs required by their local decision policy, executable skill instructions, legal/licensing material beside retained dependencies/assets, machine-readable schemas/provenance, and evidence still needed to reproduce a current decision.
7. Raw supplied evidence remains subordinate to operator/product authority. Derived reports are deleted once accepted claims and source citations are in canonical owners.
