# I2: Inbox Unidentified scope leaves once the Unidentified item resolves

## Aim

Implement the diagnosed change in
`C:/Users/Alex/Documents/GitHub/pegasus/1609sprint/email-status-updating/report.md` §1–§5 exactly.
All line anchors there were re-verified on dev (±1 line). Rule: a retained message is in the
Inbox Unidentified scope while its classification is not `classified` AND its receipt's
Unidentified item has not resolved. An unclassified message with no Unidentified item yet stays in
scope. Resolution of any kind leaves the scope; the message keeps its classification record and
Case association and remains in All incoming and on the Case. No data repair.
