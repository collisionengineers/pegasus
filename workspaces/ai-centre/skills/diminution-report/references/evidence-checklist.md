# Evidence Checklist

> **Source-workspace boundary:** Retain evidence completeness prompts, state they are experimental and not a product acceptance gate. This is evidence, an example, a package-local format, or an experiment only; `Pegasus.Core`, current operator authority, and an authorised human own every accepted fact, cost, category, outcome, legal position, send, report issue, and approval.


Read this before drafting any claimant-side diminution report.

## Required core evidence

- Instruction scope: report, solicitor advice, or market-stigma review.
- Vehicle identity: registration, VIN if available, make, model, derivative, age, mileage, ownership,
  service history, and notable specification.
- Incident evidence: accident circumstances, damage description, repair estimate, repair invoice,
  engineer report, and photographs before and after repair.
- Repair evidence: whether panels were repaired or replaced, whether work involved structural,
  welded/bonded, suspension, steering, SRS, ADAS, or safety systems, and whether the repairer was
  manufacturer-approved.
- History evidence: HPI/write-off markers, previous repairs, disclosed prior damage, purchase
  provenance, owner statement, and dealer/approved-used history.
- Market evidence: pre-incident valuation, post-repair valuation if available, comparable adverts,
  specialist/classic evidence where relevant, and guide evidence where appropriate.
- Disclosure assumption: what a reasonable seller would disclose and what a buyer/history check would
  reveal.

## Gap handling

- If repair quality is disputed but there is no post-repair inspection or photo set, state that a
  physical-quality conclusion is limited.
- If market value is central and no valuation evidence is supplied, use `vehicle-valuation` before
  stating quantum.
- If prior repair history is alleged without evidence, keep it as an unsupported allegation rather
  than adjusting the value automatically.
- If the report is for court use and expert qualifications/instructions are missing, ask for them or
  draft a non-court advice note.

## Do not infer

- Do not infer structural involvement from cost alone.
- Do not infer clean lifetime history from absence of evidence alone.
- Do not infer a fixed percentage diminution from repair cost/PAV.
- Do not infer post-repair market value without market or expert evidence.
