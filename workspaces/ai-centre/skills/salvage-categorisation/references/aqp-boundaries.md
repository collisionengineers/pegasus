# AQP Boundaries

> **Source-workspace boundary:** Retain qualification/escalation guidance as experiment metadata, not a substitute for an authorised AQP. This is evidence, an example, a package-local format, or an experiment only; `Pegasus.Core`, current operator authority, and an authorised human own every accepted fact, cost, category, outcome, legal position, send, report issue, and approval.


Use this before presenting a salvage category recommendation.

## Role boundary

An AQP or appropriately qualified engineer owns final category judgement where policy, compliance, or
disposal consequences matter. The agent can organise evidence and propose reasoning, but must not
pretend to replace the qualified decision maker.

## Training-material boundary

The AQP pre-assessment questionnaire is technical background. It is not a category matrix and should
not be cited as the method for allocating Cat A/B/S/N.

## Missing evidence language

Use direct wording:

> On the evidence supplied, the likely category is [category], subject to confirmation of [missing
> evidence]. I would not finalise the category until [inspection/diagnostic/measurement] is available.

## Review triggers

Escalate for human/AQP review where:

- structural evidence is ambiguous
- HV battery, water, fire, or contamination risk is present
- Cat A or B is under consideration
- motorcycle frame/fork/headstock evidence is incomplete
- the category is being disputed by an insurer, salvage agent, or policyholder
