# Fire, Water, Smoke, And Motorcycle Cases

## Fire and smoke

Check:

- burn extent and location
- heat exposure to structure, glass, wiring, restraint systems, fuel/HV systems
- smoke contamination and interior material damage
- whether reusable parts can be safely identified

Severe fire or contamination may point toward Cat A/B rather than ordinary S/N. Localised fire may
still require structural/HV checks before category can be finalised.

## Water and contamination

Check:

- fresh water, salt water, sewage, flood, or unknown contamination
- water level and duration
- engine, interior, wiring, control modules, HV battery, and safety systems
- odour, mould, corrosion, and hygiene risk

Do not treat "water damaged" as one category. The water source, depth, affected systems, and
contamination risk drive the reasoning.

## Motorcycles

Check:

- frame, headstock, forks, swingarm, wheels, brake assemblies, engine cases, controls, and fairings
- alignment and measurement evidence
- whether safety-critical components can be verified or must be replaced

Motorcycle category reasoning is highly evidence-sensitive. If frame/fork/headstock status is
uncertain, state the needed inspection before finalising.
