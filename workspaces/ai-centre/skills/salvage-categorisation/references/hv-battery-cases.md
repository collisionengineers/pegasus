# High-Voltage Battery Cases

Use for BEV, PHEV, and hybrid vehicles.

## Evidence to gather

- vehicle type and HV battery location
- impact location relative to HV battery, cables, inverter, charge port, coolant circuits
- warning lights, thermal events, coolant leaks, water exposure, underbody damage
- manufacturer inspection/quarantine guidance
- diagnostic results and isolation/power-down evidence
- whether battery case, fixings, seals, or cooling system are damaged

## Reasoning

- The 2025 ABI Code carries a dedicated HV-battery matrix distinguishing structural and
  non-structural battery configurations (with illustrative diagrams). Whether the battery is a
  structural member of the exact vehicle changes the S/N analysis — check the current code's
  matrix for the configuration before finalising.
- HV risk can affect safe handling, quarantine, repairability, and salvage route.
- Do not classify solely because the vehicle is electric or hybrid.
- Do not clear HV risk from exterior photos alone where the impact is near battery/cable routes or
  there is water/fire/underbody evidence.
- If HV battery integrity is uncertain, state that the category remains provisional pending
  manufacturer-method inspection/diagnostics.

## Handoff

- Use `total-loss-assessment` for power-down, quarantine, diagnostic, and repair-cost lines.
- Use `manufacturer-methods-evidence` where current OEM battery inspection rules are needed.
