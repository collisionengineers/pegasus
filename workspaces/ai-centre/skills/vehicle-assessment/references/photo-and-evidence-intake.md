# Photo And Evidence Intake

> **Source-workspace boundary:** Retain unique evidence labels/checklist; say it is an experimental intake taxonomy, not a product requirement. This is evidence, an example, a package-local format, or an experiment only; `Pegasus.Core`, current operator authority, and an authorised human own every accepted fact, cost, category, outcome, legal position, send, report issue, and approval.


What to record, what to request, and how to classify what was supplied — before any conclusion
is drafted.

## Instruction intake fields

Record what the instruction actually gives, and note the gaps. Do not stall the assessment for
missing admin fields — list them under missing evidence and proceed on what exists.

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Instructing party and their reference | Who the pack answers to; report framing |
| Claimant / driver / policyholder | Whose vehicle and whose loss |
| Vehicle: registration, VIN, make/model/derivative | Identity — verify via `vehicle-history-check` |
| Incident date and location | Date-scoped rates, weather/road context, history cross-check |
| Incident description / impact direction | Impact-path reasoning starts here |
| Damage described by owner/repairer | Owner-reported items take "we understand" wording |
| Driveability and where the vehicle is held | Roadworthiness, storage, inspection logistics |
| VAT status of the claimant | Settlement arithmetic |
| Repairer (if nominated) and estimate (if any) | Rate basis, transcription vs fresh scope |
| Hire in place / urgency | Prioritisation and storage-duration pressure |
| Any output requests beyond the standard deliverables (dispute response, addendum, handoffs, PDF opt-out) | Note them — the deliverables rule lives in SKILL.md; a PDF opt-out never removes the estimate table |

## Photo checklist

Request (or note as missing) the set that lets a desktop assessment stand up:

- All four corners, plus straight-on front, rear, and both sides.
- Close **and** context views of each damaged area — a close-up without a locating shot cannot be
  placed; a wide shot without a close-up cannot be graded.
- Registration plate, VIN plate, and instrument cluster (mileage + warning lights).
- Wheel, tyre, alloy, and suspension detail on any impacted corner.
- Interior: dashboard warning lights, airbag/pretensioner state, seatbelt condition.
- Underbonnet, boot floor, door apertures, and shut gaps where impact energy may have travelled.
- Underside where structural, water, or fire concerns exist.
- Any existing estimate, invoice, engineer brief, or incident description as documents.

Poor evidence is a finding, not a blocker: state what the supplied photos cannot show and ask for
the specific missing views.

## Evidence labels

Label each observation as you catalogue:

- `visible evidence` — directly shown in a photo or document.
- `reasonable inference` — follows from the impact path or a known linkage (state the reasoning).
- `requires further evidence` — cannot be confirmed or excluded from what was supplied; name the
  evidence that would resolve it (photo angle, strip, measurement, diagnostic scan, document).

These photo-level labels feed the pack-level source labels in `source-governance.md`.

## Evidence stages

State which stage the evidence represents, and do not present an earlier stage as a later one:

`desktop review → strip inspection → measurement/geometry → methods lookup → repair →
refinish → calibration → final QC`

If dismantling identifies additional damage, it is recorded as supplementary evidence — not
assumed into the first desktop view, and not silently merged into the original scope.

## Document intake

For each supplied document record: what it is, who produced it, its date, and whether it is
`official/live source`, `case evidence`, or a third-party assertion. An opponent's estimate is
case evidence of their position, not of the damage.

## Privacy handling

- Supplied photos commonly contain registrations, faces, locations, reflections, and EXIF/GPS
  data. Use them for the assessment; do not republish them beyond what the assessment requires.
- Never copy real registrations, VINs, addresses, phone numbers, or claim references into
  examples, fixtures, or reusable material — synthetic identifiers only.
- If a photo's EXIF/GPS or background is itself evidentially relevant (location dispute), say so
  and let the engineer decide how it is handled.
