# Structural And Alignment Evidence

When exterior photographs stop being enough, what geometry evidence to request, and how to word
the structural position without overclaiming.

## Structural vs cosmetic — the caution

Do not use exterior photos alone to conclude structural alignment, roadworthiness, or
repairability where impact energy may have entered structure or running gear. The question is
never "does the panel look bad" — it is "where did the load go".

Escalate from cosmetic to structural reasoning when any of these appear:

- Impact at or near a corner, wheel, sill, pillar, subframe, or suspension mounting zone.
- Shut lines or apertures that differ from the mirror side.
- Wheel stance, setback, or ride-height asymmetry.
- Kinked, creased, or buckled inner panels, chassis legs, or reinforcements in any photo.
- Glass cracked away from the contact point.
- Underbody scrape or deformation evidence.
- Owner reports of pulling, wandering, vibration, or "doesn't drive straight" (case evidence —
  "we understand" wording).

## Geometry evidence to request

Name the specific evidence that would resolve the structural question:

- **Datum / three-dimensional measurement** — body dimensions against manufacturer or recognised
  reference data (jig/bench or electronic measuring).
- **Diagonals** — comparative diagonal checks across apertures and platform points.
- **Apertures** — door, screen, and tailgate aperture dimensions and symmetry.
- **Wheelbase and track** — side-to-side comparison; setback on an impacted corner.
- **Four-wheel alignment report** — toe, camber, castor, SAI/KPI, thrust angle. Alignment is
  damage evidence, not just post-repair QC: a thrust-angle or castor anomaly after a corner
  impact is structural signal.
- **Ride height** — measured, side to side.

Before alignment figures are relied on, confirm tyre pressures and condition, bearing play,
wheel run-out, ride height, and vehicle loading — bad inputs make good geometry look bad.

## Reading the results

- Record reference value, observed value, variance, and significance.
- State whether variance is adjustable (within adjustment range, no distortion) or indicates
  displaced/deformed structure or running gear.
- A vehicle can measure within tolerance and still have operated an energy-management zone —
  crush initiators and deformation evidence still matter for repair method and category.

## Consequences

- Confirmed structural displacement changes the job: methods lookup
  (`manufacturer-methods-evidence`), bench/measurement operations in scope, possible salvage
  routing (`total-loss-and-salvage-routing.md`, `salvage-categorisation`), and AQP review per
  `aqp-competence-boundaries.md`.
- Standard wording while unconfirmed: *"On the present evidence, structural involvement can
  neither be confirmed nor excluded. Geometry evidence — [datum / diagonal / aperture /
  four-wheel alignment] — is required before the repair scope and category position can be
  finalised."*
