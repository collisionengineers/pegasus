# Total Loss And Salvage Routing

> **Source-workspace boundary:** This file is package-local routing evidence, not threshold, charge, or category authority, a Pegasus caller, policy, current instruction, or acceptance authority. `Pegasus.Core` and an authorised human own accepted economics and outcomes.


Repair economics, thresholds, and provisional salvage reasoning — and where this skill hands
off. The first rule: **repair economics and salvage category are separate questions.** Cost
decides whether repair is economic; damage character decides the category. Never derive one
from the other.

## PAV

The pre-accident value comes from the engineer's instruction or from `vehicle-valuation`
(evidenced by live comparables) — never invented, never estimated from memory. Where guide
screens exist without an engineer-adopted PAV, state the ratio against each guide value as
case evidence and name the adopted PAV as the missing item. Only when no candidate value
exists at all does the repair total stand alone, with PAV named as missing evidence. Never the
inverse: a ratio or threshold claim without a costed repair total is not evidence — a
total-loss position is evidenced by a completed line-by-line estimate compared to PAV, never
by a magnitude guess.

## Repair/PAV thresholds

- Typical instruction thresholds treat a vehicle as a total-loss candidate when repair costs
  reach **66–80% of PAV** — the applicable threshold is the instructing party's, not a legal
  constant; state which is in play.
- Some claims-management instructions (QDOS is the known example) set an **80% PAV ceiling**.
  The ceiling caps authorisation — what may be agreed — never the costing. Complete the
  line-by-line estimate regardless; crossing the ceiling is the finding: state the repair
  total, the ceiling arithmetic against each candidate PAV, and the ratio, then let the
  engineer decide (`estimate-construction.md`, `gotchas.md` item 9).
- Borderline ratios are a finding: state the ratio, the sensitivity (what single renewal or
  supplementary finding tips it), and the outstanding evidence.
- On repairable outcomes challenged as total losses — and the reverse — use
  `addendum-and-dispute-response.md`.

## Salvage value

- Salvage value reduces the settlement on a total loss (settlement = PAV less salvage, per the
  CE total-loss report convention).
- **Open-market salvage value and an insurer-contracted salvage rate are different bases** —
  never blend them into one figure; state which basis a figure is on.
- Provider-specific retention/deduction matrices (e.g. AX) apply only when the instruction says
  that provider's matrix governs — they are not market evidence and are not packaged in this
  skill.
- The salvage / total loss administration charge and storage lines in `extras-package.md`
  apply on total-loss outcomes.

## Provisional salvage-category reasoning

This skill may reason provisionally about category to set expectations, with the gaps named:

- Structural damage evidence present and repair economic → Cat S territory; structural
  condemnation itself needs strip/measurement and AQP review.
- Non-structural damage only, uneconomic to repair → Cat N territory.
- Fire, flood, or severe damage raising parts-reuse and safety questions → Cat B/A territory
  and immediate escalation — never concluded from photos.
- Final category is always `salvage-categorisation` + AQP work — this skill's wording stays
  "provisional", "category-indicative", "subject to AQP review".

Do not let a category proposition leak into the economics: a TPI asserting "already a total
loss" or "prior Cat marker" is answered with documentary evidence via `vehicle-history-check`,
not accepted from assertion.

## Storage and roadworthiness implications

- Unroadworthy, insecure, airbag-deployed, glazing-out, or HV/fire/water-risk vehicles justify
  the storage charge while held (see `damage-cataloguing.md` phrase bank and
  `extras-package.md` conditions).
- A vehicle held pending a category or repair decision accrues storage — long decision times
  are a real cost; note the daily rate and the decision dependency.

## Handoffs

| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| PAV or post-repair value needed | `vehicle-valuation` |
| Final category, category dispute, salvage-rate dispute, AQP support | `salvage-categorisation` |
| Prior total-loss marker / Cat history asserted | `vehicle-history-check` for the marker facts |
| Post-repair loss-of-value question | `diminution-report` / `diminution-rebuttal` — flag only, do not compute |
| Taxi/private-hire compliance report after assessment | `roadworthy-report` |
| Audatex-format EVA PDF of the assessment | Rendering of this skill's estimate — see the SKILL.md deliverables rule; `total-loss-assessment` when it is the sole ask |
