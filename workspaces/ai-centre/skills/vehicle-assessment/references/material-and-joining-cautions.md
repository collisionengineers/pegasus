# Material And Joining Cautions

Where the material decides the method. Where aluminium, UHSS, bonded panels or composite
structures are involved, **current manufacturer repair data decides permitted repair,
sectioning, joining and heat limits** — route to `manufacturer-methods-evidence`; never give
generic advice in these zones.

## High-strength and ultra-high-strength steel (HSS/UHSS)

- Do not make generic heat or pull assumptions. UHSS members are typically replace-only, with
  manufacturer-specified joints; heat can destroy the steel's properties invisibly.
- Reject any estimate line implying heating, pulling, or straightening a UHSS/boron member
  without current method support.
- Sectioning locations, joining methods (spot weld counts, MIG-braze, rivet-bond), and
  weld-through primer requirements come from the current method sheet only.
- UHSS work has cost consequences the estimate should carry (e.g. boron drill bits — see the
  ABP parts charges).

## Aluminium

- Keep in scope: cross-contamination control (separate tooling and bay), galvanic corrosion at
  mixed-metal joints, heat sensitivity, and the repairer's aluminium capability/approval.
- Aluminium panels work-harden and do not respond like steel; repairability limits are tighter
  and manufacturer method governs.
- Joining is typically bonding and riveting, not welding — single-use rivets and adhesives are
  genuine parts/materials lines, not padding.
- Aluminium construction generally supports the prestige labour rate — state the standard note
  from `labour-rates.md` where applied.

## Plastics

- Identify the substrate before any repair opinion (see `repair-renew-decision-matrix.md` for
  the full plastics decision detail).
- Substrate-specific preparation (adhesion promoters, flexible additives) belongs in the
  refinish scope where a plastic repair is priced.

## GRP / composites

- Visible fracture understates composite damage: ask for evidence of delamination, bond-line
  fracture, and damage travelling along the load path.
- Structural composite repair is manufacturer-method territory; cosmetic laminate repair is a
  finish-standard and economics question.

## Adhesives, sealers, and protection systems

Repair scope is not complete at "panel + paint". Where a repair disturbs them, these are
legitimate scope items:

- Seam sealer along joints and flanges.
- Cavity wax in enclosed sections and rails.
- Underbody coating and stone-chip protection.
- Glass bonding systems (and the glass moulding/lifting-tape consumables).
- Weld-through primer and backside protection on welded joints.
- Sound-deadening pads where panels are renewed.

*"A repaired panel is not complete unless corrosion protection, seam sealing and cavity
protection disturbed by the repair have been reinstated."* — see
`refinish-and-corrosion-protection.md` for the reinstatement checklist and
`extras-package.md` for the matching ABP lines.
