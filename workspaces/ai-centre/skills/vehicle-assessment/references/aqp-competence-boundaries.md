# AQP Competence Boundaries

> **Source-workspace boundary:** This file is package-local evaluation evidence, not AQP authority, a Pegasus caller, policy, current instruction, or acceptance authority. An authorised engineer/AQP owns every accepted domain conclusion.


What this skill may conclude on its own, what stays provisional pending engineer/AQP review, and
the escalation triggers. The AQP (Approved Qualified Person under the ABI Code of Practice)
framework is used here as **scope and evaluation guidance, not technical authority** — check
whether a newer IAEA syllabus exists before treating details as current.

## The boundary

This skill assists with evidence collation, structured observation, and provisional reasoning.
Where a conclusion has compliance, safety, or category consequences, the final decision remains
with the engineer/AQP:

| Skill may | Stays with engineer/AQP |
|---|---|
| Catalogue damage, label evidence, reason impact paths | Final salvage category (Cat A/B/S/N) |
| Provisional repair/renew and repair-scope views | Structural condemnation of a member or shell |
| ABP rate/charge position from maintained data | HV make-safe, isolation, and quarantine decisions |
| Provisional salvage-category reasoning with gaps named | Roadworthiness certification |
| Draft dispute/addendum wording for review | Statement-of-truth / Part 35 declarations |
| Identify method-lookup needs | Reliance on a specific OEM method without current-source verification |

## AQP competence coverage

The AQP competence areas below are the checklist of what a full categorisation assessment is
expected to be literate in. Use them as coverage prompts — if a case turns on one of these and
the evidence is thin, that is an escalation, not a guess:

- Body construction and materials (steel grades, aluminium, composites) and joining methods.
- ICE fundamentals: engine, cooling, turbocharging, fuel, exhaust/catalysts, air conditioning.
- Steering, suspension, wheel geometry, tyres, brakes and ABS.
- EV/HV and hybrid safety: batteries, isolation, charging systems.
- Safety and restraint systems; ADAS.
- Plastics identification and repair limits.
- Motorcycles and HGVs as distinct assessment classes.
- The ABI Salvage Code, categorisation exercises, and DVLA notification responsibilities.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate to engineer/AQP review (and the relevant specialist skill) when any of these are live:

- Confirmed or suspected structural damage — pillar, sill, chassis leg, floor, subframe
  mounting zones (`salvage-categorisation`).
- EV/HV battery involvement, thermal risk, or isolation uncertainty.
- Fire or flood/water immersion cases.
- Motorcycle or HGV subject vehicles.
- Salvage category determination, or a category/rate dispute.
- Vehicle identity concerns — VIN/registration mismatch, clone/export markers
  (`vehicle-history-check`).
- Non-reusable safety parts and waste-control questions (Cat B parts recovery boundaries).
- Any operation that requires a current OEM/Thatcham method lookup before a position can be
  taken (`manufacturer-methods-evidence`).

## Wording the boundary

State the boundary plainly in the pack: *"This is a desktop assessment. The [category /
structural / HV] position above is provisional and requires [AQP review / strip and measurement
/ manufacturer procedure] before reliance."* Do not soften the boundary to make the pack read
more complete than the evidence allows.
