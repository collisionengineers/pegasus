# Blending And Painted Sensors

Use for paint blend disputes, adjacent-panel blending, bumper/sensor painting, and radar/parking
sensor finish challenges.

## Corolla rear blend pointer

Maintained pointer: 2019-2022 Toyota Corolla rear blend method material exists in the ingest archive.
Use it only for that model/year repair context unless current source verification supports broader
use.

Reasoning:

- Adjacent-panel blending may be method-supported where colour transition, panel position, and repair
  area make a direct edge finish unacceptable.
- Blend lines should be linked to the damaged/repaired panel relationship, not added generically.

## Painted sensor pointer

Maintained pointer: Tesla park assist sensor painting material exists. Use it as a warning that
painted sensors may have manufacturer limits affecting repair, paint thickness, replacement, or
calibration.

## Query response guardrail

Do not say "blending is always required". Say the available method/paint evidence supports blending
for the specific panel relationship, subject to current manufacturer or paint-system verification.
Do not generalise one model's blend method to another vehicle without source support.
