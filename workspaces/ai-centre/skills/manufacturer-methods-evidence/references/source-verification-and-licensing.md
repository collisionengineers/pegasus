# Source Verification And Licensing

Read this before using any method pointer.

## Copyright boundary

Manufacturer, Thatcham, and repair-manual material is normally copyrighted. This skill stores
distilled pointers only. Do not reproduce diagrams, detailed procedures, measurements, cut lines,
weld counts, or full method text in outputs or runtime references.

`method-index.json` is the owned structured index for maintained pointers. It is still pointer-only:
an entry means the skill has safe lookup metadata, not that the method is approved for the live
vehicle without current-source verification.

## Current-source verification

Before relying on a method externally, verify:

- exact make, model, model year, body style, and variant
- exact component and repair section
- current version/date of the OEM or authorised source
- whether the source is repairer-access controlled or public
- whether superseded methods, technical bulletins, or market-region differences apply

## Safe wording

Use:

> The available manufacturer-method pointer indicates that this operation may depend on the exact
> section, material, joining method, and current OEM procedure. The operative method should be checked
> against the current manufacturer repair data before the line is accepted or removed.

Avoid:

- quoting procedure steps
- giving dimensions/tolerances
- saying "manufacturer says" without current verification
- using one model/year rule for another vehicle
