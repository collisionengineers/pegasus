# Tesla Model 3 Method Pointers

Use for Tesla Model 3 repair-method disputes. Verify current Tesla repair data before external
reliance.

## Rear quarter and structural body repairs

Maintained pointer: Model 3 rear quarter outer/body procedure material exists in the ingest archive.
Use it as a pointer that rear quarter sectioning/outer assembly work is method-dependent, not a
generic repair/replace choice.

Check before relying:

- exact quarter section and affected structure
- cut/section location and whether the proposed repair matches it
- body measurements and alignment evidence
- joining process, corrosion protection, seam sealing, and post-repair requirements
- whether frame bench or measurement equipment is required for the damage pattern

## Doors, wing, lamps, and adjacent components

Maintained pointers exist for front door, rear door, front wing, tail-lamp seals, and replacement
parts position. Use them to identify whether a line needs source verification rather than to quote a
method.

## Park assist sensors and painted components

Tesla parking sensor painting/source material is a high-risk dispute pointer. Painted sensor,
calibration, or replacement decisions should be verified against the current source for that sensor
and bumper/component.

## Tyres and replacement parts

Tesla tyre-pair and replacement-parts position material can support a query that asks why a tyre,
pair, or replacement part was specified. Do not generalise beyond Tesla Model 3 without verification.
