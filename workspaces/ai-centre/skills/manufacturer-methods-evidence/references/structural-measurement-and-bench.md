# Structural Measurement And Bench

Use for disputes about structural repair setup, body measurement, frame bench need, and whether a
repair can proceed without a bench.

## Maintained source families

- Tesla Model 3 body measurements.
- Tesla structural repairs without frame bench.
- Tesla rear quarter structural/body method material.
- JLR Jaguar XF (2013) body repair material: general body, corrosion protection, vehicle-specific
  tolerance checks, front-end sheet metal, and SRS/crash-sensor/airbag module coverage.
- VW Golf Variant (2014/2017) general exterior body repair material (Edition 06.2019).
- Toyota collision repair manual material with defined panel replacement-method codes and
  sectioning locations (Supra JZA80 exemplar).
- Multi-manufacturer seam-sealer/corrosion-protection matrix (whether seam sealing is bundled into
  renew times per manufacturer).
- Thatcham identification of safety-related items in body repair.

## Evidence to check

- damaged structural member or aperture
- datum/measurement point affected
- suspension/subframe mounting location
- panel gaps, shut lines, and alignment
- whether the repair method requires measurement before, during, or after repair
- whether the proposed repair setup matches current OEM instructions

## Reasoning

Do not treat "no frame bench" as "no structural verification". The general OEM position (Tesla is
the maintained exemplar) is that structural work requires approved bench mounting **unless** the
current procedure identifies a defined exception — and exceptions may still require prescribed
support setup (suspension removal or disconnection, sub-frame support, jack stands). A manufacturer
method may permit a repair without a bench in a defined case while still requiring measurements,
fixture checks, or specific joining/corrosion procedures.

Sectioning is method-and-location specific: where an OEM manual defines replacement-method codes
and cut locations for a panel, a sectioning repair proposed anywhere else is unsupported until the
current method is checked. Seam sealing and corrosion protection can be bundled into renew times
for some manufacturers and chargeable for others — check the maintained matrix pointer before
accepting or disputing a seam-sealer line. Where structural classification itself is disputed,
Thatcham's identification of safety-related items is the neutral anchor for what counts as
safety-critical in body repair.

## Safe external wording

> The structural repair line cannot be assessed from cost alone. The appropriate repair setup depends
> on the exact affected section and the current manufacturer method, including any measurement,
> alignment, joining, corrosion-protection, and post-repair checks.
