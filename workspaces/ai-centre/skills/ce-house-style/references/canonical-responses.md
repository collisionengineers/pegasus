# Canonical Query Responses

> **Source-workspace boundary:** This file is package-local evidence or an experiment only; it is not a Pegasus caller, policy, current instruction, or acceptance authority. `Pegasus.Core`, current operator authority, and an authorised human own every accepted fact, cost, category, outcome, legal position, send, report issue, and approval.


Backbone scripts for common TPI/insurer challenges. Fill `[bracketed]` figures from the case and
use the relevant specialist skill for the technical position before drafting. Keep the independence
line where appropriate. Add only what the specific query needs - do not paste these blind.

---

## TPI wants a total loss; the vehicle is repairable

> We have reviewed the repair economics and do not accept that the vehicle must be treated as a total loss on the evidence presently provided. The repair costs are **[X]%** of the assessed pre-accident value, so the claim remains a repairable proposition on the figures in our report. [The repairs have also been agreed on a contract basis, meaning the repair costs are fixed and cannot increase.] We confirm that we have no financial interest in the settlement of this claim and see no reason to deviate from our independent report.

*(Drop the "contract basis / fixed costs" sentence if repairs were not agreed on that basis. The
same script answers the variant claim that the vehicle "was already a total loss" — the repair-cost
percentage of PAV is the answer in both cases.)*

---

## Retail repair — defending the specification

> We have reviewed the repair specification and remain satisfied that the operations and charges are supported by the assessment evidence. The specification is based on the visible damage, required repair method, current ABP retail/non-contract charge guidance where applicable, and the work required to return the vehicle to its pre-accident condition. We have not been provided with specific contrary engineering evidence showing that the specification is excessive. We confirm that we have no financial interest in the settlement of this claim and see no reason to deviate from our repair specification.

---

## Salvage — standard response

> We have reviewed the salvage position and consider the salvage figure in our report to be fair and reasonable having regard to the vehicle value, repair costs, damage evidence, and salvage category applied. The third-party insurer has no entitlement to the salvage of the vehicle, and a contracted salvage rate may reflect bulk arrangements or salvage-agent agreements that are not available to the claimant in the open market. On the evidence presently provided, we see no reason to deviate from our report.

---

## Salvage — final response

> We have reviewed the salvage position in full and our conclusion remains unchanged. The salvage figure in our report has been independently assessed having regard to the vehicle value, damage evidence, category position, and repair costs of **[X]%** of the vehicle value. We do not accept that a third-party insurer's contracted salvage arrangements should be imposed as the claimant's open-market salvage position. We will reconsider the position only if new material evidence is provided.

---

## General dispute — close

Where no specific template applies, close with:

> We have reviewed the matter in full and stand by the conclusions in our report. We confirm that we have no financial interest in the settlement of this claim and see no reason to deviate from our independent report.
