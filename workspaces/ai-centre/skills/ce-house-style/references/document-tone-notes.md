# Document-Type Tone Notes

> **Source-workspace boundary:** This file is package-local evidence or an experiment only; it is not a Pegasus caller, policy, current instruction, or acceptance authority. `Pegasus.Core`, current operator authority, and an authorised human own every accepted fact, cost, category, outcome, legal position, send, report issue, and approval.


Per-document register, length defaults, and sign-off rules.

---

## Repairable / Contract Repair Report

- **Register:** measured technical third person; factual, clinical, evidence-led.
- **Length:** 3–8 pages typically.
- **Opening:** "Dear Sirs / FAO …" + "RE:" matter line.
- **Structure:** Nature of Incident → Vehicle Details → Engineer's Comments (damage by area) → Repair Specification → Settlement.
- **Sign-off:** Yours faithfully / [Engineer name] / Independent Automotive Engineer, Collision Engineers Ltd. Add qualification line (M.Inst.AEA etc.) and AQP number.
- **Tone note:** Do not editorialize damage — describe what was found and what work is required. Let the repair spec speak for itself.

---

## Total Loss Report

- **Register:** same as Repairable; additional sections for valuation and salvage.
- **Length:** 8–16 pages.
- **Key sections:** Nature of Incident → Vehicle Details → Pre-Accident Value → Repair Costs → Total Loss Determination → Salvage Value → Settlement.
- **Valuation language:** "assessed retail market value" — never "guide value", "uplift", or "EVA figure" in the external document.
- **Sign-off:** as above. Some multi-page reports include a running head with Our Ref / Your Ref / Date on continuation pages.

---

## Market Valuation Evidence

- **Register:** neutral, evidence-led. Comparable adverts are described factually; assessed value is stated with brief justification.
- **Length:** 2–4 pages.
- **Tone note:** Do not argue against the assessed retail value in the core commentary. Close firmly with the conclusion — do not undercut it with hedging.
- **External wording:** "comparable retail market evidence", "relevant live market examples", "evidence supportive of the assessed retail value". Never "cherry-picked", "highest adverts found", "selected to increase value".
- **Sign-off:** Yours faithfully / Collision Engineers Ltd (no named individual required).

---

## Addendum Report

- **Register:** formal, collegial. Opens by referencing the original report explicitly.
- **Opening line:** "This addendum is to be read alongside our original report dated [date] and supersedes nothing contained within it."
- **Purpose:** answer a specific challenge or question. Stays focused — does not re-litigate the original report in full.
- **Sign-off:** as Repairable Report.

---

## Diminution Rebuttal

- **Register:** plain-spoken professional — engineer's voice, not lawyer's. State the conclusion, then back it up.
- **Address:** FAO: The Instructing Solicitor. Never addressed directly to the Court unless specifically instructed.
- **Sign-off:** Yours faithfully / Collision Engineers Ltd. No named individual. No Statement of Truth. No qualifications. No CPR reference.
- **Tone note:** Make the case once, clearly. Avoid triple-hedging. "That cannot be right" is stronger than "that cannot, on any rational view, be correct."

---

## Roadworthy / Criminal Reports

- **Register:** formal compliance language. Statements are factual and specific.
- **Legal status lines** are fixed: "The vehicle appears roadworthy and fit for use based on a visual assessment…"
- **Sign-off:** named engineer with full qualifications and AQP number. Typically: "[Name] M.Inst.AEA, AQP Unique Identifier [ID]."
- **Caveat line (required):** "The examination was of a visual external nature based on the information provided and no dismantling or material testing was carried out…"

---

## Part 35 Responses

- **Register:** formal, direct, structured as Q&A.
- **Each answer:** brief, unambiguous, cited where needed. Do not expand beyond the question asked.
- **Independence line:** include somewhere in the response, typically as a closing statement.
- **Sign-off:** as Repairable Report.

---

## Fee Note

- **Register:** business-formal invoice. No narrative beyond the service description and payment terms.
- **Footer:** swap the standard email footer for the VAT registration number.
- **Sign-off:** Collision Engineers Ltd (no named individual).

---

## Governance reminder

All report types are AI-assisted **drafts** until a named human engineer signs off. House-style formatting does not authorise a send. Signature images (`assets/signatures/`) are placed above the engineer's typed name.
