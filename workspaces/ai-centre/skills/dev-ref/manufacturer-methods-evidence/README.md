# manufacturer-methods-evidence

Organises manufacturer repair-method evidence into safe, paraphrased decision pointers — repair scope, wheel/tyre replacement, alloy refurbishment, blending, painted sensors, structural measurement, bench/setup, and OEM-method dispute responses — **without reproducing copyrighted OEM procedures, diagrams, or step text**.

## What it provides

- **`SKILL.md`** — the workflow and the copyright-safe wording rules.
- **`references/`** — per-method-area guidance plus the method index.
- **`scripts/`** — method-index validation.

## Uses

`total-loss-assessment` (when the method affects the estimate), `ce-house-style` (insurer/TPI response wording), and `vehicle-valuation` (where the method affects repairability, category, provenance, or value).

## Status

Production-ready; packaged in normal dist builds.

## Layout

`README.md` and `AGENTS.md` live in this `-dev` wrapper (never uploaded); the uploadable skill is the nested `manufacturer-methods-evidence/` folder. See `AGENTS.md` for maintenance notes.
