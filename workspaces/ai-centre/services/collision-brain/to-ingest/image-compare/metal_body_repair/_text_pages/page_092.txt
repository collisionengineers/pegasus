METAL BODY REPAIR - OD1653 - LESSON 2/TASK 2
(6) Cleaning and Painting.
CAUTION
Radiators   should   never   be   painted   with   ordinary
lacquers, enamels or paints as these insulate the heat
transfer.     This   reduces   the   cooling   efficiency.
Always use non­insulating radiator core paint.
After all leaks have been repaired, the radiator should be painted.  A non­
insulating paint should be used.   Before painting, the radiator should be
cleaned, dry and free of all flux residue.
NOTE
No   more   than   one   coat   of   paint   will   be   applied   to
radiator cores on tactical wheeled vehicles which are
subject to painting.
