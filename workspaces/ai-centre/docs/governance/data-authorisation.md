# Data-use authorisation

- **Recorded:** 21 July 2026
- **Authority:** Collision Engineers, confirmed directly by the repository owner
- **Status:** Granted

Collision Engineers granted evaluation and development use of the named private corpus and,
subject to separate exact-target access approval, relevant Box and Outlook material. This record
does not authorise repository inclusion, publication, bulk archive import, live-system access, or
external transfer.

Private material remains outside the repository under the ignored, immutable `corpus/` boundary.
Box and Outlook archives remain in their source systems. Any bounded evaluation extract must retain
source custody, provenance, case isolation, and the repository's source-role rules; generated
evaluation results belong under `artifacts/`.

The authorisation does not make every source statement authoritative or every artifact suitable for
every model task. Pipelines must still preserve provenance, sender/source role, client and case
boundaries, report version, evidence cutoff, retention/deletion lineage, and third-party licence
metadata so outputs can be interpreted correctly.

Credentials, tokens, passwords, private keys, malware, and executable payloads are not training data.
The known `Manufacturers.ods` credential sheet must remain blocked from ingestion and its credentials
should be rotated. That security control does not limit use of the rest of the authorised archives.

This is data-use permission, not authority to send email, alter a live case system, issue or sign a
professional report, deploy a service, or incur external cost. Those operational actions retain their
separate approval gates.
