# P00 — governance, specifications, licensing and provenance

## Scope

Own the authoritative five-format boundary, exact primary-reference registry, `EXT-*` catalogue, compatibility vocabulary and evidence/provenance rules. Research outside the five-format boundary cannot create product work.

## Owned units

- `EXT-GOV-001` product scope, specification registry and evidence governance.
- `EXT-LIC-001` dependencies, source/oracle/fixture provenance, licensing and notices.

## Required outputs

- Accepted architecture and scope decisions with explicit supersession history.
- A specification/version record for every active feature.
- One compatibility matrix tied to `EXT-*` units and evidence records.
- Machine-readable provenance and fixture manifests with safe path handling.
- Distribution gates for dependencies, copied/derived source, fixtures and notices.

## Exit evidence

P00 is accepted only when scope and status cannot be inferred differently from different documents, every active artefact has an owner/provenance route, and an authorised reviewer has resolved the applicable distribution decisions. Mapping alone does not approve copied code, fixtures or packaging.
