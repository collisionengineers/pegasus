# Structural And Non-Structural Checklist

Use this for Cat S versus Cat N reasoning.

## Structural indicators

Check for damage to:

- chassis legs, longitudinal members, crossmembers, crash structures
- sills, A/B/C pillars, roof rails, floor pan, bulkhead, inner wings, quarter inner structures
- bonded/welded load-bearing panels or apertures
- suspension mounting points, steering mounting points, subframe mounts
- monocoque alignment, datum points, or body measurement deviations
- safety-cell structures affected by airbag/SRS deployment or intrusion

If structural involvement is suspected but not proven, say what inspection, measurement, strip, or
method evidence is needed.

## Non-structural indicators

Usually Cat N territory where economic salvage is otherwise appropriate:

- bolt-on panels, bumpers, lamps, grilles, trims
- cosmetic outer-skin damage without load-bearing structure
- mechanical/electrical items not affecting structural integrity
- glazing, wheels, tyres, suspension components where mounting structure is not damaged
- theft, vandalism, electrical, or interior damage without structural compromise

## Evidence quality

Strong evidence:

- stripped photos, measurements, repair method notes, engineer/AQP inspection, geometry readings

Weak evidence:

- exterior photos only, generic estimate labels, assumptions from repair cost, unsupported insurer
  assertion

Do not finalise Cat S from weak evidence alone.
