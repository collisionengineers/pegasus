# Physical Versus Market Condition

Use this where a party argues that no visible defect means no diminution, or that paint-depth
readings are mandatory before any market-stigma opinion can be given.

## Distinction

- **Physical condition** asks whether the vehicle has been repaired properly and whether defects can
  be detected on inspection.
- **Market condition** asks whether a reasonable buyer would discount the vehicle because of the
  accident/repair history, disclosure, provenance, or perceived risk.

A vehicle can be physically well repaired and still have some market-history stigma. Conversely, a
poor repair may justify a repair-quality issue rather than inherent diminution.

## Paint-depth and inspection

- Paint-depth readings can help assess repair consistency, prior repairs, and panel history.
- Paint-depth readings do not prove the absence of all prior cosmetic work over the vehicle's life.
- Mixed steel, aluminium, composite, and plastic substrates require correct probe selection and
  method disclosure.
- If relying on readings, identify gauge, probe type, calibration, substrate, panel map, raw readings,
  photos, and interpretation.

## Drafting rule

Do not overstate either side. If the report is based on documents only, say so and state what can and
cannot be concluded from the material reviewed.
